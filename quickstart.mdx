# Quick Start

This guide gives you an overview of the recommended path for getting started with COD King.

<Info>
  Exact dashboard navigation and configuration steps will be added after the relevant COD King screens and workflows are verified.
</Info>

## Before you begin

Make sure you have access to the Shopify store where COD King will be used and the permissions required to manage the app.

## Recommended setup path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install and open COD King">
    Add COD King to the Shopify store and open the application from your Shopify environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the COD features you need">
    Decide which workflows are relevant to your store, such as OTP verification, partial COD, COD fees, COD rules, or COD-to-prepaid incentives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure one workflow at a time">
    Start with the feature that addresses your most important COD requirement. Review its settings carefully before enabling it for customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the customer experience">
    Verify your configuration using an appropriate test flow before relying on it for live orders.
  </Step>
</Steps>
