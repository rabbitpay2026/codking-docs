## Collaborate Store with COD King

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access your collaboration settings in Shopify" id="collaborate">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload your screenshot here */}

    - Log in to your Shopify Admin account.
    - Click **Settings** from the bottom-left corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your collaboration code" id="collaboration-code">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload your screenshot here */}

    - Go to **Users** → **Security**.
    - Scroll to **Collaborators**.
    - Copy the **Collaboration Code**.
    - Share the **Collaboration Code** together with your **Store URL** with the COD King team.

    <Warning>
      Do not generate a new Collaboration Code until the COD King integration has been completed.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the collaboration request" id="accept-request">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload your screenshot here */}

    - You'll receive a collaboration request from the **COD King Developers** team.
    - Review the request.
    - Click **Accept Request**.

    <Info>
      The collaborator access is used only to install and configure COD King.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Installation of COD King App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grant permissions and install the app" id="install-app">
    After your collaboration request is accepted, COD King is installed on your store from a secure installation link.

    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Shopify installation screenshot here */}

    - Once you accept the collaboration request, we'll send you the installation link.
    - Click the link and follow the prompts to grant the necessary permissions.
    - Click **Install** to complete the app installation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Get Started with COD King App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the COD King app" id="open-app">
    Once installed, COD King appears alongside your other apps in the Shopify Admin.

    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Shopify Apps screenshot here */}

    - Open your Shopify Admin account.
    - Click **Apps** from the left sidebar.
    - Open the **COD King** application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your store" id="store-setup">
    The onboarding wizard collects the business details COD King needs before it can process orders.

    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the onboarding wizard screenshot here */}

    Complete the following sections to finish setting up your store:

    - Business Type
    - Business Details
    - Business Team
    - Payment Setup

    <Note>
      Add your GST details, including GSTIN, to claim GST returns.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Complete the Initial Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure your COD workflows" id="initial-setup">
    Select the modules you want to enable for your store. Here's what each one covers:

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="OTP Verification" icon="shield-check">
        Confirm a customer's phone number with a one-time password before the COD order is accepted.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="COD Rules" icon="filter">
        Decide which carts and customers are allowed to use Cash on Delivery.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Partial COD" icon="credit-card">
        Collect an advance payment on COD orders to reduce RTO and minimise fake orders.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="COD Fees" icon="receipt">
        Apply a fee to COD orders and steer customers toward prepaid checkout.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Notifications" icon="message">
        Set up the customer messages sent across the COD order lifecycle.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Integrations" icon="puzzle-piece">
        Connect the other tools and providers COD King works with.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>

    <Info>
      If you didn't configure these right after installing the app, you can still set them up later from your COD King dashboard.
    </Info>

    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the COD King settings screenshot here */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore app features and settings" id="explore-settings">
    - Review the key features available within the app.
    - Adjust settings to tailor the app to your business requirements.
  </Step>
</Steps>
