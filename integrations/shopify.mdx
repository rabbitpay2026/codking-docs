## Collaborate Store with COD King {#collaborate-store}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access your collaboration settings in Shopify" id="collaborate">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload your screenshot here */}

    - Log in to your Shopify Admin account.
    - Click **Settings** from the bottom-left corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your collaboration code" id="collaboration-code">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload your screenshot here */}

    - Go to **Users** → **Security**.
    - Scroll to **Collaborators**.
    - Copy the **Collaboration Code**.
    - Share the **Collaboration Code** together with your **Store URL** with the COD King team.

    <Warning>
      Do not generate a new Collaboration Code until the COD King integration has been completed.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the collaboration request" id="accept-request">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload your screenshot here */}

    - You'll receive a collaboration request from the **COD King Developers** team.
    - Review the request.
    - Click **Accept Request**.

    <Info>
      The collaborator access is used only to install and configure COD King.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Installation of COD King App {#installation}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grant permissions and install the app" id="install-app">
    After your collaboration request is accepted, COD King is installed on your store from a secure installation link.

    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Shopify installation screenshot here */}

    - Once you accept the collaboration request, we'll send you the installation link.
    - Click the link and follow the prompts to grant the necessary permissions.
    - Click **Install** to complete the app installation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Get Started with COD King {#get-started}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the COD King App" id="open-app">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Shopify Apps screenshot here, wrapped in a Frame */}

    - Open your Shopify Admin account.
    - Click **Apps** from the left sidebar and open **COD King**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your store" id="store-setup">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the onboarding screenshot here, wrapped in a Frame */}

    Complete the following sections to finish setting up your store:

    - Business Type
    - Business Details
    - Business Team
    - Payment Setup

      Add your GST details, including GSTIN, to claim GST returns.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Complete Pre-Integration Setup {#pre-integration-setup}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete payment gateway setup" id="initial-setup">
    Configure the payment gateways you intend to use before continuing with your COD King setup. Select the gateways you want to enable for each **Payment Method**. If you already have **active accounts** with any of the gateways below, here's what each one needs:

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Razorpay" icon="credit-card">
        Requires account authorization from your Razorpay dashboard. **No webhook setup is needed.** [View setup →](/integrations/payment-gateways/razorpay)
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Cashfree" icon="credit-card">
        Credentials are available in your Cashfree dashboard. **Webhook configuration is required.** [View setup →](/integrations/payment-gateways/cashfree)
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="PayU" icon="credit-card">
        Credentials are available in your PayU dashboard. **Webhook configuration is required.** [View setup →](/integrations/payment-gateways/payu)
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="PhonePe" icon="credit-card">
        Share your merchant ID with the COD King team. Mapping is handled manually by our team. [View setup →](/integrations/payment-gateways/phonepe)
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Easebuzz" icon="credit-card">
        Credentials are shared by the Easebuzz team. **Webhook configuration is required.** [View setup →](/integrations/payment-gateways/easebuzz)
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Paytm" icon="credit-card">
        Credentials are available in your Paytm merchant dashboard. **Webhook configuration is required.** [View setup →](/integrations/payment-gateways/paytm)
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="XPay" icon="credit-card">
        Credentials are available in your XPay dashboard. **Webhook configuration is required.** [View setup →](/integrations/payment-gateways/xpay)
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>

    <Note>
      If you don't configure payment gateways during the initial setup, you can configure them later from the COD King dashboard.
    </Note>

    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Payment Settings screenshot here, wrapped in a Frame */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect marketing and analytics tools" id="marketing-tools">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Marketing and Analytics Settings screenshot here, wrapped in a Frame */}

    Connect the marketing platforms you intend to use with your Shopify store. Examples include:

    - Meta Pixel
    - Google Analytics
    - Google Ads

    For detailed setup instructions, refer to the [Analytics documentation](/integrations/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore COD King features and settings" id="explore-settings">
    - Review the available COD King features.
    - Configure settings according to your business requirements.
    - Familiarize yourself with available configuration options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your Shopify theme for integration" id="select-theme">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Shopify Themes screenshot here, wrapped in a Frame */}

    - Tell the COD King team which Shopify theme should be integrated.
    - If no theme is specified, your primary published theme will be used for the integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Theme Setup & Go Live {#theme-setup-go-live}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preview and verify the integration" id="go-live">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Theme Preview screenshot here, wrapped in a Frame */}

    - After confirming your Shopify theme, the COD King team will prepare a preview/testing version.
    - Review the preview carefully to verify that the checkout and COD experience work as expected.
    - Report any required changes before requesting the final deployment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and go live" id="final-setup">
    {/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — upload the Go Live screenshot here, wrapped in a Frame */}

    Once you have verified the preview, notify the COD King team that everything looks correct. After confirmation, the integration will be moved to your selected live Shopify theme.
  </Step>
</Steps>
