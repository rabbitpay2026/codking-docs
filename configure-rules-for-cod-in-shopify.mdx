### 1.  What it does ?

COD Show/Hide allows you to:

- Show COD only for selected **pincodes**
- Hide COD for **risky locations**
- Allow COD only above/below **specific order amounts**
- Disable COD for **specific products or collections**

### 2.  Why is COD Show/Hide Needed ?

- **Reduce RTOs:** Restrict COD for high-risk or unsuitable orders.
- **Control COD Eligibility:** Ensures COD is offered only where your courier supports it
- **Improve Order Management:** Apply automated rules without manually checking every order.
- **Optimize Delivery Costs:** Minimize unnecessary shipping and return expenses.
- **Increase Order Quality:** Avoids misuse by customers with a history of cancellations.

### 3.  How Is It Beneficial for Businesses ?

- **Reduce RTOs:** Restrict COD for high-risk orders and customers to minimize failed deliveries.
- **Lower Delivery Costs:** Prevent unnecessary shipping and return expenses.
- **Improve Payment Control:** Encourage prepaid payments for selected orders or locations.
- **Manage COD Efficiently:** Automatically apply COD rules without manual intervention.
- **Increase Order Quality:** Offer COD only when the order meets your defined criteria.

### 4.  Key Features

- **15\+ Rule Parameters:** Create highly flexible rules using 15\+ parameters to control when and where COD is shown or hidden.
- **Phone & Email Blacklisting:** Prevent COD orders from specific customers by blacklisting their phone numbers or email addresses.
- **Complete Flexibility, Zero Latency:** Configure COD visibility exactly as needed without adding delays to the customer’s checkout experience.

**As you can see below, the Cash on Delivery shipping method has been successfully hidden**.

**Hide Payment Method Based on Cart Value:**

- Use this option to hide COD when the customer’s cart value is below or above a certain amount.
- Example: Minimum cart value: **100 INR and** Maximum cart value: **1000 INR**
- If a customer’s cart value is **below 100 INR** or **above 1000 INR**, the COD payment option will not be shown

<Frame>
  ![Cart Value](/images/cart-value.png "Cart Value")
</Frame>

**Show or Hide Payment Method Based on Location (Country)**:

- Here, you can choose the countries where COD should be available.
- Example:<br />If you select **India**, COD will be visible only for customers in India.<br />For all other countries, the COD option will be automatically hidden.

  ![Location](/images/Location.png "Location")
</Frame>

**Hide Payment Method for Selected Product IDs**

To disable COD for specific products:

1. Add the **Product IDs** in the provided box.
2. Save the settings.

Customers will not see COD as an option for these specific products.

<Frame>
  ![Product](/images/product.png "Product")
</Frame>

**Hide Payment Method for Selected Product Variant IDs:**

If you want to hide COD only for certain **variants** within a product:

1. Add the **Variant IDs** in the given box
2. Save the settings

COD will be hidden only for those specific variants.

<Frame>
  ![Variant](/images/variant.png "Variant")
</Frame>

**Hide Payment Method for Selected Phone Numbers:**

You can hide COD for specific **customer phone numbers**.

If you notice repeated fake orders or cancellations from particular numbers:

1. Enter those phone number
2. Save the settings

Customers using those numbers will **not be able to place COD orders**.

**Hide Payment Method for Selected Email Addresses:**

You can hide COD for specific **customer email addresses**.

If you notice repeated fake orders or cancellations from particular email addresses :

1. Enter those email addresses
2. Save the settings

Customers using those email addresses will **not be able to place COD orders**.

**Hide Payment Method for Selected Delivery Methods:**

If your store has multiple delivery/shipping methods, you can hide COD for specific ones.

Example:<br />If you select a delivery method from the dropdown, COD will not appear when the customer chooses that specific delivery option on the checkout page.

### 6.  Sample Use Cases

- **High Order Value:** Hide COD for orders above a specific value to reduce payment risk.
- **Specific PIN Codes:** Offer COD only in serviceable or low-RTO locations.
- **Customer History:** Hide COD for customers with repeated cancelled or undelivered orders.
- **Product Type:** Disable COD for high-value, fragile, or customized products.
- **Prepaid Promotion:** Hide COD for selected products or campaigns to encourage prepaid payments.

<Warning>
  Use the COD Show & Hide rules carefully, as incorrect configurations may hide Cash on Delivery for eligible customers or make COD available to customers who should be restricted. Make sure all conditions, rule priorities, and customer criteria are configured correctly before enabling the feature.
</Warning>
