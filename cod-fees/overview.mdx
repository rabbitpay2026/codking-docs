# COD Fees

COD fees provide merchants with a way to apply an additional charge to eligible Cash on Delivery orders.

## Overview

The appropriate COD fee strategy depends on your store's requirements and the conditions supported by your COD King configuration.

<Warning>
  Do not configure live fees based on documentation examples until the exact COD King dashboard options have been verified.
</Warning>
