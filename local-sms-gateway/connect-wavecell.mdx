title: "Connect Wavecell (8x8 Connect) to COD King"
description: "Learn how to configure Wavecell (8x8 Connect) as a Local SMS Operator in COD King."
---

Wavecell is now part of **8x8 Connect**. You can use 8x8 Connect to send SMS notifications through COD King.

This guide explains how to get the required credentials from 8x8 Connect and add them to COD King.

## What You Need

Before you start, make sure you have:

- An 8x8 Connect account
- Access to your 8x8 Connect dashboard
- Company information for Sender ID registration
- Access to your COD King admin panel

---

## Step 1: Log in to 8x8 Connect

Open the 8x8 Connect login page:

[https://connect.8x8.com/login](https://connect.8x8.com/login)

Log in to your account.

After logging in, you will see the 8x8 Connect dashboard.

## Step 2: Create an API Key

From the left-side menu, open **API Keys**.

Click **Create API key**.

Enter a name for the API key.

For example:

`COD King SMS Integration`

Then create the API key.

**Important:** Do not share your API key or include the full key in screenshots.

## Step 3: Get the Sub Account ID

On the **API Keys** page, find the SMS Subaccount that you want to use.

Copy the **Subaccount ID**.

For example:

`BirdCh_OTP`

Your Subaccount ID may be different.

> <Frame>
>   ![Untitled](/images/Untitled.png "Untitled")
> </Frame>

You will enter this value in COD King's **Sub Account ID** field.

## Step 4: Register a Sender ID

From the 8x8 Connect menu, open:

**Sender ID → Registrations**

Click **Register new sender ID**.

  ![Screenshot 2026 09 08 123422](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-08-123422.png "Screenshot 2026 09 08 123422")
</Frame>

Select the destination country where you will send SMS messages.

## Step 5: Add Company Details

8x8 Connect will ask for your company and business information.

Enter your actual:

- Company name
- Signing officer details
- Company website
- Company address
- Country and city
- Other required business information

> <Frame>
>   ![Screenshot 2026 09 08 124440](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-08-124440.png "Screenshot 2026 09 08 124440")
> </Frame>

Upload the documents requested by 8x8 Connect.

**Use your real company information and documents. Do not use example information when submitting the registration.**

---

## Step 6: Add Your Sender ID

Enter the Sender ID that you want to use for sending SMS.

For example:

`CODKING`

Review the information and submit the registration.

Depending on the destination country, the Sender ID may need to be approved before it can be used.

---

## Step 7: Open COD King

Once you have your:

- **Sub Account ID**
- **API Key**
- **Sender ID**

Log in to your Shopify admin and open **COD King**.

Go to:

**Local SMS Operators**

> <Frame>
>   ![Screenshot 2026 09 08 170346](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-08-170346.png "Screenshot 2026 09 08 170346")
> </Frame>

Under **Network Provider**, select:

**Wavecell (Global)**

> <Frame>
>   ![Screenshot 2026 09 08 170617](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-08-170617.png "Screenshot 2026 09 08 170617")

## Step 8: Enter Wavecell Details

Enter the details obtained from 8x8 Connect.

| COD King Field | Enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Sub Account ID** | Your 8x8 SMS Subaccount ID |
| **API Key** | Your 8x8 API Key |
| **Sender ID** | Your registered/approved Sender ID |

Make sure all three values are entered correctly.

Then click **Save**.

---

## Step 9: Test SMS

After saving the configuration, trigger an SMS notification from your Shopify store.

For example, you can place a test order and check whether the configured SMS is delivered.

You can also check **SMS → Logs** in 8x8 Connect to verify the message.

## Wavecell Configuration Summary

To connect Wavecell with COD King, you need these three values:

| Required Value | Where to Get It |
| --- | --- |
| **Sub Account ID** | 8x8 Connect → API Keys |
| **API Key** | 8x8 Connect → API Keys |
| **Sender ID** | 8x8 Connect → Sender ID |

Once these details are entered and saved in COD King, Wavecell (8x8 Connect) is ready to send SMS notifications.
