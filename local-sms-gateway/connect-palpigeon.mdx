PalPigeon (Turkey) is available as a local SMS operator in COD King. You can configure the integration through the Local SMS Gateway settings in your Shopify admin.

This guide explains how to obtain the required credentials, configure PalPigeon in COD King, and test the integration.

## Step 1: Create a PalPigeon Account

To get started, you need an active PalPigeon account that supports SMS messaging.

Contact PalPigeon or your COD King representative to obtain the provider's official account registration instructions.

You may need to provide contact information, business details, and other information requested by the provider.

For information about COD King's supported local SMS operators, see the [COD King Local SMS Gateway page](https://codking.tech/features/messaging-gateways).

> **Note:** Confirm the account registration requirements and activation process directly with PalPigeon before proceeding.

## Step 2: Obtain Your Login Credentials

After your PalPigeon account has been activated, obtain the credentials required for the COD King integration.

You will need:

- **Username**
- **Password**

Ask PalPigeon to confirm that these credentials are valid for SMS integration with COD King.

Keep your credentials secure and do not share them publicly.

## Step 3: Obtain Your Sender ID

A Sender ID is the name or identifier displayed as the sender of an SMS message.

Contact PalPigeon to obtain or register a Sender ID for your account.

Before continuing, confirm that:

- Your Sender ID is approved for SMS sending.
- It is supported for your destination country.
- You know the exact Sender ID value to enter in COD King.

Use the Sender ID exactly as provided by PalPigeon.

> **Note:** Confirm any Sender ID registration requirements and restrictions with the provider.

## Step 4: Configure PalPigeon in COD King

In your Shopify admin, open:

**Apps → COD King → Settings → Local SMS Gateway**

Select **PalPigeon (Turkey)** from the **Network Provider** dropdown.

Enter the following details:

### Username

Enter the username provided by PalPigeon for your SMS integration.

### Password

Enter the password provided by PalPigeon.

### Sender ID

Enter the Sender ID registered or approved for your account.

Check that all three values are correct, then save the operator configuration.

## Step 5: Test Your Integration

After saving your PalPigeon configuration, go to the **Test Your Integration** section.

Enter a valid recipient phone number in the **Phone Number** field, including the country code.

For example, a Turkish mobile number may be entered in international format:

`+905XXXXXXXXX`

Click **Send Test SMS** to send a test message.

Check the recipient's phone to confirm whether the message was delivered successfully.

If the test message is delivered successfully, your PalPigeon integration is configured correctly and ready to use.

### Testing Tips

- Save the operator configuration before testing.
- Verify the Username and Password with PalPigeon if authentication fails.
- Use the Sender ID approved for your account.
- Enter a valid recipient phone number with the country code.
- Confirm that your PalPigeon account is active and has sufficient SMS credits.
- If the test fails, contact PalPigeon to check the account status and delivery restrictions.

## Need Help?

If you need assistance creating your PalPigeon account, obtaining credentials, registering a Sender ID, or troubleshooting SMS delivery, contact PalPigeon through its official support channel.

For help with the COD King configuration, visit the [COD King Contact Us page](https://codking.tech/contact).
