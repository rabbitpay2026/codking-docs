# Partial COD

Partial COD allows a store to combine an upfront payment with the remaining amount handled through the Cash on Delivery journey.

## Why use Partial COD?

Merchants may use an advance payment requirement as part of their COD strategy while still offering Cash on Delivery to eligible customers.
