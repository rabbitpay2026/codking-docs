## Overview

OTP Verification enables merchants to validate customer intent before processing Cash on Delivery (COD) orders. By sending a one-time password (OTP) via SMS or WhatsApp, only verified orders are allowed to proceed further in the fulfillment process.

This feature plays a critical role in minimizing fake orders, reducing Return to Origin (RTO), and improving overall operational efficiency.

---

## Business Context

COD orders often introduce challenges such as:

- Fraudulent or non-serious buyers
- High RTO rates
- Increased logistics and operational costs

OTP Verification introduces a verification layer at checkout, ensuring that only genuine customers confirm their orders.

## How It Works

1. A customer places a COD order on the Shopify store
2. An OTP is automatically sent via SMS or WhatsApp
3. The customer enters the OTP to confirm the order
4. The system validates the OTP in real time
5. The order is marked as "Verified" and proceeds to fulfillment

---

## Key Features

### Multi-Channel OTP Delivery

Supports OTP delivery through:

- SMS
- WhatsApp<br />This improves delivery success rates and ensures broader reach.

### Real-Time Verification

- Instant OTP validation
- No delay in order confirmation

### Fraud Prevention

- Filters out fake, bot, or invalid orders
- Ensures only genuine transactions are processed

### Retry and Resend Mechanism

- Allows customers to request OTP again if not received
- Reduces drop-offs caused by delivery issues

### Conditional Triggering

OTP verification can be applied selectively based on:

- Order value thresholds
- Geographic locations
- COD-specific orders

## Benefits

- Reduction in Return to Origin (RTO) rates
- Lower logistics and operational costs
- Improved order success rate
- Enhanced customer trust and reliability
- More controlled and secure COD operations

## Use Cases

### High COD Volume Stores

Verify all COD orders to ensure authenticity and reduce losses.

### High-Risk Regions

Enable OTP verification selectively for locations with higher RTO rates.

### High-Value Orders

Apply verification for expensive products to reduce financial risk.

### Step 1: Enable OTP Verification

- Navigate to the COD King dashboard
- Open the "OTP Verification" section
- Enable the OTP feature

### Step 2: Select Delivery Channels

- Choose SMS, WhatsApp, or both

### Step 3: Define Rules (Optional)

- Configure conditions based on:
  - Order value
  - Customer location
  - Product category

### Step 4: Customize Message Template

- Add brand identity
- Modify OTP message content

### Step 5: Save and Activate

- Save configuration changes
- OTP verification becomes active for applicable orders
