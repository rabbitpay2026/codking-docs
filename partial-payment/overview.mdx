# Partial Payment

Partial Payment allows you to collect a fixed amount or percentage from customers before confirming a Cash on Delivery (COD) order. The remaining amount is collected by your courier partner at the time of delivery.

This helps merchants reduce Return to Origin (RTO), eliminate fake COD orders, improve cash flow, and increase order commitment without completely removing the COD payment option.

## How It Works

1. Customer adds products to the cart.
2. Customer proceeds to checkout.
3. RabbitPay evaluates all configured Partial Payment rules.
4. If the order matches a rule, the customer is asked to pay the configured advance amount.
5. The advance payment is collected securely.
6. The remaining amount is automatically marked as Cash on Delivery.
7. The order is placed successfully.

---

## Why Use Partial Payment?

Partial Payment is designed to improve order quality while maintaining the convenience of Cash on Delivery.

### Benefits

- ✅ Reduce fake COD orders
- ✅ Lower Return to Origin (RTO)
- ✅ Improve customer commitment
- ✅ Increase prepaid revenue
- ✅ Improve cash flow
- ✅ Reduce logistics losses
- ✅ Continue offering COD to genuine customers
- ✅ Build customer trust with secure advance payments

## Features

### Collection Based Rules

Enable Partial Payment only for selected collections.

Example:

- Electronics
- Luxury Products
- Furniture
- High RTO Products

---

### Product Based Rules

Apply Partial Payment only for selected products.

Perfect for:

- Expensive items
- Customized products
- Limited edition products
- High-risk SKUs

---

### Pincode Based Rules

Control Partial Payment using delivery pin codes.

Examples:

- High RTO locations
- Remote areas
- Blacklisted locations
- State-wise configuration

---

### Customer Based Rules

Create rules based on customer information.

Examples:

- New Customers
- Returning Customers
- VIP Customers
- Tagged Customers

---

### Cart Value Rules

Configure Partial Payment based on order value.

Examples

| Cart Value | Advance |
| --- | --- |
| ₹0 - ₹999 | No Partial Payment |
| ₹1000 - ₹4999 | ₹200 |
| ₹5000\+ | 20% Advance |

---

### Fixed Amount

Collect a fixed advance amount.

Example:

Order Value

₹4,500

Advance

₹500

Remaining COD

₹4,000

---

### Percentage Based Payment

Collect a percentage of the order value.

Example

Order Value

₹5,000

Advance

20%

Customer Pays

₹1,000

Remaining COD

₹4,000

---

### Multiple Rules

Create multiple Partial Payment rules for different scenarios.

Example

- Rule 1 → Luxury Products → 20%
- Rule 2 → Electronics → ₹500
- Rule 3 → Delhi → 10%
- Rule 4 → New Customers → ₹300

RabbitPay automatically applies the matching rule.

---

### Enable / Disable Rules

Each rule can be enabled or disabled instantly without deleting it.

This makes testing and seasonal campaigns easier.

---

### Rule Priority

If multiple rules match the same order, RabbitPay evaluates them according to their configured priority.

This ensures predictable behavior for every checkout.

## Best Practices

- Use percentage-based payment for expensive products.
- Use fixed amounts for low-value orders.
- Configure pincode rules for high-RTO regions.
- Test every rule before making it live.
- Avoid collecting unnecessarily high advance payments.
- Review analytics regularly and optimize your rules.
- Keep your rules simple and easy to manage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I collect a fixed amount?

Yes.

You can collect any fixed amount before allowing Cash on Delivery.

---

### Can I collect a percentage instead?

Yes.

You can collect any percentage of the order value.

---

### Can I create multiple rules?

Yes.

There is no restriction on creating multiple Partial Payment rules.

---

### What happens if multiple rules match?

RabbitPay applies the rule according to the configured priority.

---

### Can I disable a rule without deleting it?

Yes.

Every rule can be enabled or disabled instantly.

---

### Does Partial Payment work with COD?

Yes.

Customers pay only the advance amount online, while the remaining balance is collected by the courier during delivery.

---

### Can I use Partial Payment with COD Rules?

Absolutely.

Partial Payment works seamlessly with COD Rules, COD Fees, OTP Verification, and other RabbitPay checkout features.
