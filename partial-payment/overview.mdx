## 1. What is Partial COD?

**Partial Cash on Delivery (Partial COD)**—also referred to as **Upfront Payment, Deposit Payment, or Part Payment**—is a hybrid checkout solution designed for e-commerce stores.

Instead of forcing buyers to choose between 100% prepaid or 100% cash on delivery, partial COD splits the order total into two parts:

- **Upfront Deposit (Paid Online):** A small fixed fee (e.g., ₹99, \$5) or a percentage (e.g., 10%–20%) collected online at checkout via UPI, cards, net banking, or digital wallets.
- **Remaining Balance (Paid on Delivery):** The remaining balance collected in cash or digitally by the delivery agent upon package arrival.

---

## 2. Why Partial COD is Needed

Traditional 100% cash on delivery is a major driver of losses for e-commerce businesses in emerging markets. Below are the primary structural challenges resolved by partial payment:

- **High Return to Origin (RTO) Rates:** Up to 20%–40% of 100% COD orders are rejected at the doorstep due to buyer impulse, changed minds, or unavailability, resulting in heavy reverse logistics fees.
- **Lack of Customer Intent:** Zero upfront financial commitment encourages casual ordering. Requiring even a nominal deposit filters out non-serious buyers.
- **Sunk Shipping Costs:** When a COD order is returned, merchants bear forward shipping, return shipping, and inventory lock-up costs. Upfront deposits guarantee shipping cost coverage.
- **Dead Inventory Lockup:** Rejected COD packages spend days or weeks in transit and return logistics, preventing merchants from selling items to real buyers.

<Tip>
  **The Psychology of the Upfront Deposit:** When a customer pays as little as \$2 or ₹99 upfront, their cognitive commitment shifts from _“I’ll decide when it arrives”_ to _“I have already invested in this purchase.”_ Doorstep refusal drops exponentially.
</Tip>

## 3. Countries Where Partial Payment is Needed

Partial COD is especially impactful in high-COD emerging e-commerce economies across Asia, MENA, and Eastern Europe:

| Country / Region | Local Market Conditions | Ideal Partial COD Strategy |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **India (IN)**<br /> | High COD usage in Tier 2/3 cities; seamless UPI ecosystem (GPay, PhonePe). | Charge a small flat deposit (e.g., ₹99 or ₹149) to cover reverse logistics without raising conversion friction. |
| **Saudi Arabia (SA) & UAE**<br /> | High Average Order Value (AOV); COD heavily favored alongside BNPL. | Use a percentage-based deposit (15%–20%) to mitigate high international or cross-border shipping risks. |
| **Philippines (PH)** | Cash-driven market supported by GCash and PayMaya. | Set tier-based rules requiring a 20% deposit on high-ticket goods and a flat PHP 100 on lower carts. |
| **Pakistan (PK)**<br /> | Over 80% COD market share; high courier non-delivery and refusal rates. | A mandatory upfront fee covering 100% of the courier shipping cost prior to dispatch. |
| **Egypt (EG)** | Cash-heavy e-commerce market; doorstep rejections strain merchant margins. | Upfront token deposit (EGP 50–100) via local digital wallets (Fawry, Vodafone Cash). |
| **Ukraine (UA)** | The postal service (Nova Poshta) cash payment model is widely adopted. | Set partial prepayment rules equal to round-trip shipping costs. |

## 4. Partial Payment Rules

The rules engine allows merchants to define precise conditions under which Partial COD is offered.

## Payment Rules

### Rule 1: Rule Configuration

- **Rule Name:** The label you give this payment option (e.g., _"Full Payment"_ or _"10% Deposit"_).
- **Amount Type:** How much money the customer pays upfront:
  - **Full Payment:** Customer pays 100% of the cost upfront.
  - **Percentage: The** customer pays a percentage upfront (e.g., 20%), and the rest on delivery.
  - **Fixed:** The customer pays a set dollar/rupee amount upfront (e.g., ₹200 deposit), and the rest on delivery.
  - **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Customer pays nothing upfront and pays the full amount upon delivery (with optional extra delivery fees)

### Rule 2: Cart Range (Advanced)

- **Min & Max Cart Range:** Set price limits for when this payment option appears. For example, if you set Min to 500 and Max to 5,000, this option will only show up if the customer's cart total is between ₹500 and ₹5,000. Leave both at `0.0` to make it apply to all cart totals.

### Rule 3: Discount Settings (Advanced)

- **Discount Code:** An optional code name (e.g., earth10) applied to reward customers who select this payment option.
- **Discount Type & Value:** Choose whether to give a percentage off (e.g., 10% off) or a flat amount off (e.g., ₹100 off) when this rule is chosen.

### Rule 4: Product Filter

Determines which products in your store trigger this payment rule:

- **All products:** The payment option is available for every single product in your store.
- **Selected products only:** The payment option appears **only if all items** in the customer's cart are on your selected list.
  - _Example:_ If your list has Product A and Product C:
    - Cart with **A \+ B**—**Hidden** (because B is not on the list).
    - Cart with **A—Shown** (because A is on the list).
- **Exclude selected products:** The payment option is hidden if the cart contains **any** product from your exclusion list.
  - _Example:_ If you exclude Product A:
    - Cart with **B \+ C**—**shown** (contains no excluded items).
    - Cart with **A \+ B**—**Hidden** (contains excluded product A).

### **Rule 5: Variant Filter (Advanced)**

Filters payment options by specific product variations, such as **size**, **color**, or **material**:

- Works together with the Product Filter. A cart must satisfy **both** the product requirements and the variant requirements for the rule to show up.
- Example: You can allow partial payments for "Large T-Shirts," but hide them for "Small T-Shirts."

### **Rule 6: Collection Filter (Advanced)**

Filters payment options by Shopify product categories/collections (e.g., "Winter Collection" or "Clearance Items"):

- The rule only applies if the items in the customer's cart belong to your specified collections.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 08 10, 07 44 26 PM](/images/screenshot-08_10,-07_44_26-PM.jpg "Screenshot 08 10, 07 44 26 PM")

### Rule 7: Match Any Product in Cart

This changes how strictly the app checks multi-item carts against your Product, Variant, or Collection filters:

- **Disabled / Unchecked (Default):** **Every single product** in the customer's cart must meet your rules. If even one item doesn't qualify, the payment option is hidden.
- **Enabled / Checked:** **At least one product** in the cart must meet your rules for the payment option to appear for the whole cart.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 08 10, 07 44 42 PM](/images/screenshot-08_10,-07_44_42-PM.jpg "Screenshot 08 10, 07 44 42 PM")

### Rule 8: Zipcode Restrictions & Zipcode Includes

Controls payment options based on the customer's delivery pincode/zip code:

- **Zipcode Restrictions:** Hides this payment option for specific zip codes (useful for turning off Cash on Delivery in areas with high return rates or delivery issues).
- **Zipcode Includes:** Shows this payment option **only** to customers in specific zip codes and hides it for everyone else.

### Rule 9: Display Settings (Advanced)

Controls how the payment option appears to shoppers at checkout:

- **Amount to show:** Overrides the automatically calculated upfront payment amount. Use this if you want to set a fixed upfront fee or add an extra fixed charge (like a ₹50 processing/shipping fee) without changing the base product price. Leaving it blank lets the app calculate the deposit automatically.

## 5. Setting Up Partial Payment

Follow this step-by-step guide to configure and launch Partial COD on your store.

### Step 1: Install and Activate the App

Navigate to the Shopify App Store directory, search for the **Codking** app, and click **Install**. Authorize app permissions to link your store catalog.

### Step 2: Configure Online Payment Gateways

Connect your preferred online payment gateway to process upfront deposits:

1. Go to **Dashboard \> Settings \> Payment Gateways**.
2. Select your gateway (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe, PayU, GCash, Tabby).
3. Enter API keys (key ID & secret key) and save settings.

<Warning>
  **Important:** Ensure your payment gateway is set to **Auto-Capture** so upfront deposits are immediately authorized and captured at checkout.
</Warning>

### Step 3: Locate the partial payment feature

Click on "Manage."

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 08 10, 03 27 49 PM](/images/screenshot-08_10,-03_27_49-PM.jpg "Screenshot 08 10, 03 27 49 PM")
</Frame>

### Step 4: Locating the theme editor

You will see an option of features right under the COD King on the APPS section on the left sidebar

An interface as below appears; click on the "open theme editor."

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 08 10, 08 20 37 PM](/images/screenshot-08_10,-08_20_37-PM.jpg "Screenshot 08 10, 08 20 37 PM")
</Frame>

<Info>
  Clicking on the theme editor will take you to your Shopify stores interface.<br />Select the range of theme as per requirement.
</Info>

### Step 5: Select the payment rules.

Go back to the Shopify tab and scroll down to find an interface for payment rule selection.

1. Full payment: select a fixed amount from the total amount for upfront payment
2. Percentage-based: select the percentage from the total amount that is desired for upfront payment.

<Info>
  Select the most suitable rule according to the needs
</Info>

Tick off the box.
