Rules decide what each option does. **Advanced Settings** decide how Partial Payment behaves across the whole store. Open **Settings → Partial payments & COD** and click the **Advanced Settings** tab next to **Payment Rules**.

<Frame>
  ![Advanced Settings tab](/images/pp-03-adv-tax.png "The Advanced Settings tab has three sections")
</Frame>

If you change something, the **Save** bar appears at the top. Click **Save** to keep it.

## Tax and calculations

| Setting | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Include tax in product calculations** | Ticked by default. Taxes are included when the upfront and later amounts are worked out. |
| **Partial Tax Text** | Text shown to explain tax when it is left for later. Default: *Taxes computed later*. |

<Tip>
  If your deposit does not include tax, change the Partial Tax Text to something clear for your customers, such as "Taxes due on delivery".
</Tip>

## Shipping configuration

<Frame>
  ![Shipping Rate Keyword field](/images/pp-04-adv-shipping.png "Shipping Configuration: Shipping Rate Keyword")
</Frame>

**Shipping Rate Keyword** is a word that identifies partial-payment shipping rates, for example `partial` or `installment`. When a customer picks a shipping rate that contains this word at checkout, COD King triggers the deposit flow.

## Payment flow

This decides what happens to the order when only part of it is paid online.

<Frame>
  ![Payment Flow with Same Order selected](/images/pp-05-adv-flow.png "Payment Flow: Same Order (Recommended) or New Order")
</Frame>

| Option | What happens | Best for |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Same Order (Recommended)** | The remaining COD amount is added to the **same order** shortly after it is placed (usually within 1–2 minutes). Example: a ₹1000 order with ₹100 paid upfront is updated to ₹900 as the COD amount. | Stores that fulfil orders manually. |
| **New Order** | The original order is **cancelled** and a **new order** is created for the COD remainder. Example: a ₹1000 order with ₹100 upfront is cancelled and replaced by a ₹900 COD order. | Stores with automatic fulfilment or a 3PL, where the order is picked up the instant it is created. |

<Warning>
  Choose carefully if you use automatic fulfilment. With **Same Order** there is a short moment before the balance is added. If your 3PL picks orders up immediately, use **New Order**.
</Warning>

## Meta Pixel and Google Ads

Both are configured outside this page, under **Settings → Integrations**, so that deposits and purchases feed your ad reporting.

- **Meta (Facebook) Pixel**: open **Meta Pixel** and enter your Pixel ID and Conversions API access token.
  <Frame>
    ![Meta Pixel card under Integrations](/images/pp-16-meta-pixel.png "Meta Pixel under Settings → Integrations")
  </Frame>
- **Google Ads**: open **Google Ads** and enter your Conversion ID and Conversion Label.
  <Frame>
    ![Google Ads card under Integrations](/images/pp-17-google-ads.png "Google Ads under Settings → Integrations")
  </Frame>
