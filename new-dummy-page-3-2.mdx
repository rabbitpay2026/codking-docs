## 1. What is Prepaid Discount?

A Prepaid Discount is an incentive offered to customers who choose to pay for their orders in advance instead of selecting Cash on Delivery (COD).

With COD King's Prepaid Discount feature, merchants can automatically offer a discount at checkout to encourage customers to switch from COD to prepaid payments, resulting in a faster, smoother, and more secure checkout experience.

## 2. Why is a Prepaid Discount Needed?

- **Reduces Return to Origin (RTO):** Encourages customers to choose prepaid payments, resulting in fewer order cancellations and delivery rejections.
- **Increases Prepaid Conversions:** Motivates customers to switch from Cash on Delivery (COD) to prepaid payment methods by offering an additional benefit.
- **Improves Order Reliability:** Prepaid orders are typically more reliable, helping merchants process and fulfill orders with greater confidence.
- **Lowers Operational Costs:** Reduces the additional costs associated with COD processing, order verification, and reverse logistics.
- **Enhances Cash Flow:** Enables merchants to receive payments immediately instead of waiting for COD settlements.
- **Boosts Customer Satisfaction:** Rewards customers with savings, creating a more attractive and seamless checkout experience.
- **Increases Overall Profitability:** Helps merchants minimize losses caused by failed deliveries while improving long-term business growth.

## 3. **How it works ?**

1. The customer adds products to the cart.
2. At checkout or Buy now, COD King opens a payment popup. Every option is listed there, and your prepaid discount is shown on the full prepaid choice.
3. When the customer picks prepaid, COD King applies the discount and continues them to Shopify checkout to complete payment.
