Cash on Delivery (COD) orders often lead to higher returns and added operational costs. **COD King’s COD to Prepaid Conversion** feature helps turn COD orders into prepaid ones, reducing losses and improving conversions. 

## **1. What is COD to Prepaid Conversion ?**

Turn COD orders into prepaid orders by giving customers a quick and secure way to pay online after placing their order.

COD King sends **WhatsApp or SMS messages** with a secure payment link, allowing customers to complete their payment instantly.

- **3–4%** average COD → Prepaid conversion rate in India
- **2% conversion fee** applies only to successful conversions
- Add a **Thank You page banner** to encourage customers to pay online
- Reduce **COD-related returns and operational costs**
- Improve **cash flow** by converting COD orders into prepaid payments

<Tip>
  Keep your payment messages short and compelling. Highlight the benefit of paying online, such as **instant confirmation, faster processing, or an exclusive discount**, to encourage more COD customers to convert.
</Tip>

## **2. Why It Matters ?**

- Over a **65% of e-commerce orders via COD**, but many are returned due to non-acceptance.
- COD Prepaid helps balance **trust and operational efficiency**, especially for new or budget-conscious buyers.

## **4. Step-By-Step Setup Guide :**

## **Step 1 : Activate COD to Prepaid Conversion**

1. Go to the **COD King Dashboard**.

<Frame>
  ![COD Prepaid 1](/images/COD-prepaid-1.png "COD Prepaid 1")
</Frame>

2. Open **COD To Prepaid** by clicking on Manage.

<Frame>
  ![Manage](/images/Manage.png "Manage")
</Frame>

3. Click **Activate COD to Prepaid Conversion**.

<Frame>
  ![Activate](/images/Activate.png "Activate")
</Frame>

This ensures customers receive the payment link immediately after placing a COD order.

## **Step 2 : Enable Checkout Integration**

This step is required for showing the conversion banner on the Thank You and Order Status pages.

### **1.  Enable COD King Extension on the Thank You Page**

- Open your Shopify **Thank You Page** settings.

<Frame>
  ![Thank You 2](/images/thank-you-2.png "Thank You 2")
</Frame>

- Go to **App Blocks** and Select **otp-cod-king-thankyou**.

<Frame>
  ![App Block](/images/app-block.png "App Block")
</Frame>

- Click **Save**.

### **2.  Enable COD King Extension on the Order Status Page**

- Open **Order Status Page settings**.

<Frame>
  ![Order](/images/order.png "Order")
</Frame>

- Go to **App Blocks**  and Select **otp-cod-king-thankyou**.

<Frame>
  ![Status](/images/status.png "Status")
</Frame>

- Click **Save**.

<Warning>
  **Tip:** Make sure the extension is enabled on **both pages** for the conversion banner to work correctly.
</Warning>

## **Step 3: Connect Your Payment Gateway**

Select your preferred payment gateway. **Razorpay** is a commonly used option.

- Select **Razorpay**.

<Frame>
  ![Razorpay](/images/Razorpay.png "Razorpay")
</Frame>

- Enter your **API Key** and **API Secret**.

<Frame>
  ![Arrow](/images/Arrow.png "Arrow")
</Frame>

- Save the configuration.

This enables COD King to generate secure payment links for COD orders.

## **Step 4: Add a Prepaid Discount**

Encourage customers to convert by offering a small discount.

- Select **Flat Amount**.

<Frame>
  ![Flat](/images/flat.png "Flat")
</Frame>

- Enter your discount value, such as **₹50.**

<Frame>
  ![Save](/images/save.png "Save")
</Frame>

- Save the settings.

## **Step 5: Set Up WhatsApp/SMS Reminders**

As per the **Meta policy**, customers cannot receive more than one marketing message within **24 hours**.

<Frame>
  ![Remind](/images/remind.png "Remind")
</Frame>

### **First Reminder**

- Choose: **Send** or **Don’t Send**
- This message goes right after the order is placed.

### **Second Reminder**

- Again choose: **Send** or **Don’t Send**
- The message will be sent **24 hours after the first one**.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 21 160249](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-21-160249.png "Screenshot 2026 08 21 160249")
</Frame>

These reminders include the payment link, encouraging customers to complete their payment instantly.

<Tip>
  **Tip:** Keep reminder messages short and benefit-focused. A small prepaid discount can make the payment link more compelling.
</Tip>

- Go to Banner settings, here we can customize what to be displayed in the Pop-up after order is placed

<Frame>
  ![Banner](/images/Banner.png "Banner")
</Frame>

- Test the flow from your online store 
- Once Pay Now button is clicked you will be redirected to another page to pay online and avail the offer
- Once payment is converted to prepaid, the initial order for COD will get cancelled and a new order will get created.

## **5. Final Result**

Once everything is set up:

- Customers placing COD orders will automatically receive the payment link.
- A banner will appear on the Thank You Page and Order Status Page.
- Customers can convert their order to prepaid with one click.
- You save on RTO (Return to Origin) and improve order reliability.
