**Customer Engagement** consists of features that help build meaningful interactions with customers through Customer notifications via Whatsapp or SMS to make their shopping journey more enhanced and informed, Hence boosting engagement and trust.

<Info>
  **Keep customers** informed and engaged. **Build brand & trust** by connecting with your customer
</Info>

Here we will discuss about the following features:

1. **Confirmation Messages**
2. **Fulfilment Messages**
3. **First Purchase Messages**

Let us understand in detail what purpose they serve and how to use/enable them.

<Warning>
  Sending WhatsApp messages to your customers needs approval.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Keep your notifications enabled and complete your settings - WhatsApp messages will start automatically once your account is approved. You can still send test notifications while approval is pending. To maintain a healthy eco-system and avoid spamming, our team will review your Store. <br />
</Info>

## 1.  **Confirmation Messages**

Helps send automated **SMS and WhatsApp notifications** to customers as soon as an order is placed, ensuring instant communication, improved customer engagement, and a seamless post-purchase experience.

**What the Customer Receives**

- A confirmation message
- Order ID
- Order summary (optional)
- Thank you note
- Brand name or store name

# Step-by-Step Guide :

### Step 1: Open Customer Engagement

Go to **COD King**  then go to **Settings** after that locate **Customer notifications** (under Notifications) from your Shopify admin panel.

<Frame>
  ![Customer notifications in COD King Settings](/images/ce-01-open-customer-notifications.png "Customer notifications in COD King Settings")
</Frame>

### Step 2: Select Confirmation Messages

In the Customer Notifications table, find the **Order placed** row to configure notifications that are sent when an order is placed.

<Frame>
  ![Order placed row](/images/ce-02-order-placed-row.png "Order placed row")
</Frame>

### Step 3: Choose the Communication Channel

Select the channel through which you want to notify customers — **SMS, WhatsApp, or both**.

<Frame>
  ![SMS and WhatsApp checkboxes for Order placed](/images/ce-03-order-placed-channels.png "SMS and WhatsApp checkboxes for Order placed")
</Frame>

### Step 4: Configure the Message

Use the two icons in the **Preview** column to review the confirmation messages in Whatsapp or SMS whcih customers will receive after placing an order. To change an SMS template, use the **Chat with us** link in the SMS preview.

<Frame>
  ![WhatsApp preview for Order placed](/images/ce-04-order-placed-whatsapp-preview.png "WhatsApp preview for Order placed")
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![SMS preview for Order placed](/images/ce-05-order-placed-sms-preview.png "SMS preview for Order placed")
</Frame>

### Step 5: Enable the Message

Review your message settings and tick the **SMS** and/or **WhatsApp** checkbox in the **Order placed** row to start sending automated notifications when orders are placed.

<Frame>
  ![Enable Order placed notifications](/images/ce-06-order-placed-enable.png "Enable Order placed notifications")
</Frame>

**Best Use Cases**

- Stores with high COD order volume
- Stores facing customer hesitation after placing orders
- Brands wanting a professional, automated communication flow

<Tip>
  Keep your confirmation message short, clear, and reassuring. Include essential order information so customers immediately know their order has been successfully placed.
</Tip>

## 2.  **Fulfilment Messages**

Allows you to send automated **SMS and WhatsApp notifications** to customers when an order is dispatched, keeping them informed about their order status and ensuring a smooth post-purchase experience.

**What the Customer Receives**

- Tracking link
- Courier partner details (if available)
- Estimated delivery message
- Order ID

# Step-by-Step Guide :

### Step 1: Open Customer Engagement

Go to **COD King → Settings → Customer notifications** from your Shopify admin panel.

<Frame>
  ![Customer notifications in COD King Settings](/images/ce-01-open-customer-notifications.png "Customer notifications in COD King Settings")
</Frame>

### Step 2: Select Fulfilment Messages

In the Customer Notifications table, find the **Order Fulfilment/Tracking** row to configure notifications for dispatched orders.

<Frame>
  ![Order Fulfilment/Tracking row](/images/ce-07-fulfilment-row.png "Order Fulfilment/Tracking row")
</Frame>

### Step 3: Choose the Communication Channel

Select the preferred communication channel — **SMS, WhatsApp, or both**.

<Frame>
  ![SMS and WhatsApp checkboxes for Order Fulfilment/Tracking](/images/ce-08-fulfilment-channels.png "SMS and WhatsApp checkboxes for Order Fulfilment/Tracking")
</Frame>

### Step 4: Configure the Message

Use the two icons in the **Preview** column to review the fulfilment message that will be sent when an order is dispatched. To change an SMS template, use the **Chat with us** link in the SMS preview.

<Frame>
  ![WhatsApp preview for Order Fulfilment/Tracking](/images/ce-09-fulfilment-whatsapp-preview.png "WhatsApp preview for Order Fulfilment/Tracking")
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![SMS preview for Order Fulfilment/Tracking](/images/ce-10-fulfilment-sms-preview.png "SMS preview for Order Fulfilment/Tracking")
</Frame>

### Step 5: Enable Fulfilment Messages

Review your message and settings, then tick the **SMS** and/or **WhatsApp** checkbox in the **Order Fulfilment/Tracking** row to **save and enable** the fulfilment notification.

<Frame>
  ![Enable Order Fulfilment/Tracking notifications](/images/ce-11-fulfilment-enable.png "Enable Order Fulfilment/Tracking notifications")
</Frame>

**Best Use Cases**

- Stores dispatching through multiple shipping partners
- Stores wanting fewer customer queries
- Businesses wanting to improve delivery performance

<Tip>
  Keep fulfilment messages concise and informative. Include key order details and dispatch information to keep customers updated throughout the delivery journey.
</Tip>

## 3.  **First Purchase Messages**

Helps you send automated **WhatsApp messages** to customers after their first order by offering exclusive discounts and timely reminders. Encourage repeat purchases, increase customer engagement, and drive higher conversions.

**What the Customer Receives**

- Welcome message
- Thank-you note
- Discount code (optional)
- Brand introduction

# Step-by-Step Guide :

### Step 1: Open Customer Engagement

Go to **COD King → Settings → Customer notifications** from your Shopify admin panel.

<Frame>
  ![Customer notifications in COD King Settings](/images/ce-01-open-customer-notifications.png "Customer notifications in COD King Settings")
</Frame>

### Step 2: Select First Purchase Messages

Scroll down the Customer Notifications table to the **First Time Purchase** row to create automated messages for customers after their first purchase.

<Frame>
  ![First Time Purchase row](/images/ce-12-first-purchase-row.png "First Time Purchase row")
</Frame>

### Step 3: Enable WhatsApp Messages

Enable **WhatsApp** as the communication channel for sending first-purchase messages to customers.

<Frame>
  ![WhatsApp checkbox and Schedule & Configure link](/images/ce-13-first-purchase-whatsapp.png "WhatsApp checkbox and Schedule & Configure link")
</Frame>

### Step 4: Add Your Offer

Click **Schedule & Configure** and enter the **Discount Value** and **Discount Code** for the **exclusive discount** offered to customers after their first order. Use the Preview icon to see the message.

<Frame>
  ![WhatsApp preview for First Time Purchase](/images/ce-14-first-purchase-whatsapp-preview.png "WhatsApp preview for First Time Purchase")
</Frame>

### Step 5: Set the Reminder

In the same **First Time Purchase Configuration** window, set **Schedule time for first time purchase** to choose the **reminder timing** for the message.

<Frame>
  ![First Time Purchase Configuration](/images/ce-15-first-purchase-schedule.png "First Time Purchase Configuration")
</Frame>

### Step 6: Save and Enable

Review the message, discount, and reminder settings, then **save and enable** the First Purchase Message.

<Frame>
  ![Enable First Time Purchase messages](/images/ce-16-first-purchase-enable.png "Enable First Time Purchase messages")
</Frame>

**Best Use Cases**

- New brands wanting to grow customer relationships
- Stores focusing on repeat buyers
- Stores with strong branding or loyalty programs

<Tip>
  Make the offer clear and time-sensitive to create urgency, encourage repeat purchases, and drive higher conversions.
</Tip>

# How Customer Engagement Helps Your Business ?

- **Builds Customer Trust:** Keeps customers informed at every key stage of their order journey.
- **Drives Repeat Purchases:** First-purchase offers encourage customers to shop again.
- **Boosts Conversions:** Timely messages and exclusive discounts motivate customers to complete more purchases.
- **Improves Customer Experience:** Delivers relevant updates through SMS and WhatsApp without manual effort.
- **Strengthens Customer Relationships:** Consistent communication helps turn first-time buyers into loyal customers.
