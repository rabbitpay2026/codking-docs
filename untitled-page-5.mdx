## Why Offer COD Fees?

Prepaid orders are simpler to manage, but COD is often what makes a store scalable in the first place:

- **Builds trust fast—new** customers who don't know your brand yet are far more comfortable ordering without handing over card details upfront.
- **Lifts conversions—in** cash-heavy markets, dropping COD can cost you 40–60% of potential sales.
- **Reaches unbanked shoppers—not** everyone has a credit card or online banking, and COD opens the door to them.
- **Cuts cart abandonment—customers** who hit a payment gateway error or just prefer paying in person won't bail at checkout.

<Info>
  **The catch:** COD also brings higher RTO (return-to-origin) rates and added courier fees. A COD fee helps you absorb that cost without giving up the sales COD brings in.
</Info>

---

## How Do COD Fees Help Your Business?

- **Covers Courier Surcharges:** Offsets the extra fees logistics partners charge for collecting and handling physical payment.
- **Reduces RTO Rates:** Customers who commit to a small extra charge are far more likely to accept the delivery, significantly reducing return-to-origin orders.
- **Protects Profit Margins:** Allows you to continue offering cash payments without letting failed deliveries eat into your bottom line.

## Setting up COD fees

### 1. Select Your COD Payment Method

- Open the dropdown under **"Select your Cash On Delivery payment method"** and select **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.
- This links COD King to your store's payment setup so it knows exactly when to apply your fee rules.

### 2. Create a COD Shipping Rate in Shopify

COD King doesn't touch your checkout code directly—instead, it reads a shipping rate you set up in Shopify that carries your COD charge.

- **Open Shipping Settings—go** to **Settings \> Shipping and Delivery** in your Shopify admin, or use the [Open Shipping Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/store/earthytones-store/apps/cod-verification/features/cod-fees) shortcut inside the app.

### 3. **Set Your Keyword**

The keyword is what connects your Shopify shipping rate to COD King's logic.

- **Default:** cash
- **How it works:** any shipping rate within its name gets recognized as a COD rate at checkout.
- **Customization:** change it to anything you like (e.g., "cod")—just make sure it matches exactly in your Shopify rate name.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 08 18, 07 33 48 PM](/images/screenshot-08_18,-07_33_48-PM.jpg "Screenshot 08 18, 07 33 48 PM")
</Frame>

### 4. Enable Cash on Delivery Fees

- Check "Enable Cash on Delivery Fees."
- From here, COD King filters checkout automatically—if a customer picks a shipping rate with your keyword, they'll only see Cash on Delivery as the payment option, and your fee applies seamlessly.

## Best Practices for Setting COD Fees

- **Keep It Fair:** Set a nominal fee (e.g., ₹50–₹200) that covers your extra expenses without discouraging potential buyers.
- **Be Transparent:** State the fee clearly at checkout so customers aren't surprised by additional charges at delivery.
- **Pair with Prepaid Discounts:** Offer a small discount on prepaid options alongside the COD fee to gently nudge customers toward paying upfront.
