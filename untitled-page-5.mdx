## What are COD fees?

Cash on Delivery lets customers pay when the order arrives at their door instead of online in advance. Depending on your courier, that payment might be collected as cash, via mobile wallet, or by card swipe at the doorstep.

---

## Why Offer COD Fees?

Prepaid orders are simpler to manage, but COD is often what makes a store scalable in the first place:

- **Builds trust fast—new** customers who don't know your brand yet are far more comfortable ordering without handing over card details upfront.
- **Lifts conversions—in** cash-heavy markets, dropping COD can cost you 40–60% of potential sales.
- **Reaches unbanked shoppers—not** everyone has a credit card or online banking, and COD opens the door to them.
- **Cuts cart abandonment—customers** who hit a payment gateway error or just prefer paying in person won't bail at checkout.

<Info>
  **The catch:** COD also brings higher RTO (return-to-origin) rates and added courier fees. A COD fee helps you absorb that cost without giving up the sales COD brings in.
</Info>

## Setting up COD fees

### 1. Select Your COD Payment Method

- Open the dropdown under **"Select your Cash On Delivery payment method"** and select **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.
- This links COD King to your store's payment setup so it knows exactly when to apply your fee rules.

### 2. Create a COD Shipping Rate in Shopify

COD King doesn't touch your checkout code directly—instead, it reads a shipping rate you set up in Shopify that carries your COD charge.

- **Open Shipping Settings—go** to **Settings \> Shipping and Delivery** in your Shopify admin, or use the [Open Shipping Settings](https://admin.shopify.com/store/earthytones-store/apps/cod-verification/features/cod-fees) shortcut inside the app.
