**Customer Engagement** consists of features that help build meaningful interactions with customers through Customer notifications via Whatsapp or SMS to make their shopping journey more enhanced and informed, Hence boosting engagement and trust.

<Info>
  **Keep customers** informed and engaged. **Build brand & trust** by connecting with your customer
</Info>

Here we will discuss about the following features:

1. **Confirmation Messages**
2. **Fulfilment Messages**
3. **First Purchase Messages**

Let us understand in detail what purpose they serve and how to use/enable them.

<Warning>
  Sending WhatsApp messages to your customers needs approval.
</Warning>

  Keep your notifications enabled and complete your settings - WhatsApp messages will start automatically once your account is approved. You can still send test notifications while approval is pending. To maintain a healthy eco-system and avoid spamming, our team will review your Store. <br />

## 1.  **Confirmation Messages**

Helps send automated **SMS and WhatsApp notifications** to customers as soon as an order is placed, ensuring instant communication, improved customer engagement, and a seamless post-purchase experience.

**What the Customer Receives**

- A confirmation message
- Order ID
- Order summary (optional)
- Thank you note
- Brand name or store name

# Step-by-Step Guide : 

### Step 1: Open Customer Engagement

Go to **COD King**  then go to **Features** after that locate **Customer Engagement** from your Shopify admin panel.

<Frame>
  ![Customer](/images/Customer.png "Customer")
</Frame>

### Step 2: Select Confirmation Messages

Under Customer Engagement, select **Confirmation Messages** to configure notifications that are sent when an order is placed and click on Manage.

<Frame>
  ![Confirm](/images/Confirm.png "Confirm")
</Frame>

### Step 3: Choose the Communication Channel

Select the channel through which you want to notify customers — **SMS, WhatsApp, or both**.

<Frame>
  ![Order Placed](/images/order-placed.png "Order Placed")
</Frame>

### Step 4: Configure the Message

Create or customize the confirmation messages in Whatsapp or SMS whcih customers will receive after placing an order. Add relevant order details or dynamic variables where required.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 19 151347](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-19-151347.png "Screenshot 2026 08 19 151347")
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 19 151426](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-19-151426.png "Screenshot 2026 08 19 151426")
</Frame>

### Step 5: Enable the Message

Review your message settings and enable the **Confirmation Message** to start sending automated notifications when orders are placed.

<Frame>
  ![Confirm Save](/images/Confirm-Save.png "Confirm Save")
</Frame>

**Best Use Cases**

- Stores with high COD order volume
- Stores facing customer hesitation after placing orders
- Brands wanting a professional, automated communication flow

<Tip>
  Keep your confirmation message short, clear, and reassuring. Include essential order information so customers immediately know their order has been successfully placed.
</Tip>

## 2.  **Fulfilment Messages**

Allows you to send automated **SMS and WhatsApp notifications** to customers when an order is dispatched, keeping them informed about their order status and ensuring a smooth post-purchase experience.

**What the Customer Receives**

- Tracking link
- Courier partner details (if available)
- Estimated delivery message
- Order ID

# Step-by-Step Guide : 

### Step 1: Open Customer Engagement

Go to **COD King → Customer Engagement** from your Shopify admin panel.

<Frame>
  ![Customer Engagement](/images/Customer-engagement.png "Customer Engagement")
</Frame>

### Step 2: Select Fulfilment Messages

Under Customer Engagement, select **Fulfilment Messages** to configure notifications for dispatched orders and click on Manage.

<Frame>
  ![Fulfill](/images/Fulfill.png "Fulfill")
</Frame>

### Step 3: Choose the Communication Channel

Select the preferred communication channel — **SMS, WhatsApp, or both**.

<Frame>
  ![SMS](/images/SMS.png "SMS")
</Frame>

### Step 4: Configure the Message

Create or customize the fulfilment message that will be sent when an order is dispatched. Add relevant order details using the available dynamic variables.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 19 153514](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-19-153514.png "Screenshot 2026 08 19 153514")
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 19 153617](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-19-153617.png "Screenshot 2026 08 19 153617")
</Frame>

### Step 5: Enable Fulfilment Messages

Review your message and settings, then **save and enable** the fulfilment notification.

<Frame>
  ![Lets Save](/images/Lets-save.png "Lets Save")
</Frame>

**Best Use Cases**

- Stores dispatching through multiple shipping partners
- Stores wanting fewer customer queries
- Businesses wanting to improve delivery performance

<Tip>
  Keep fulfilment messages concise and informative. Include key order details and dispatch information to keep customers updated throughout the delivery journey.
</Tip>

## 3.  **First Purchase Messages**

Helps you send automated **WhatsApp messages** to customers after their first order by offering exclusive discounts and timely reminders. Encourage repeat purchases, increase customer engagement, and drive higher conversions.

**What the Customer Receives**

- Welcome message
- Thank-you note
- Discount code (optional)
- Brand introduction

# Step-by-Step Guide : 

### Step 1: Open Customer Engagement

Go to **COD King → Customer Engagement** from your Shopify admin panel.

<Frame>
  ![Customer Engagement 1](/images/Customer-engagement-1.png "Customer Engagement 1")
</Frame>

### Step 2: Select First Purchase Messages

Under Customer Engagement, select **First Purchase Messages** to create automated messages for customers after their first purchase.

<Frame>
  ![First Time](/images/first-time.png "First Time")
</Frame>

### Step 3: Enable WhatsApp Messages

Enable **WhatsApp** as the communication channel for sending first-purchase messages to customers.

<Frame>
  ![Whatsapp Toggle](/images/Whatsapp-toggle.png "Whatsapp Toggle")
</Frame>

### Step 4: Add Your Offer

Create a message offering an **exclusive discount** to customers after their first order. Add the discount details and any relevant dynamic variables.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 08 19 154501](/images/Screenshot-2026-08-19-154501.png "Screenshot 2026 08 19 154501")
</Frame>

### Step 5: Set the Reminder

Configure the **reminder timing** to send a follow-up message and encourage customers to use their discount.

<Frame>
  ![Schedule](/images/Schedule.png "Schedule")
</Frame>

### Step 6: Save and Enable

Review the message, discount, and reminder settings, then **save and enable** the First Purchase Message.

<Frame>
  ![After That Save](/images/After-that-save.png "After That Save")
</Frame>

**Best Use Cases**

- New brands wanting to grow customer relationships
- Stores focusing on repeat buyers
- Stores with strong branding or loyalty programs

<Tip>
  Make the offer clear and time-sensitive to create urgency, encourage repeat purchases, and drive higher conversions.
</Tip>

# How Customer Engagement Helps Your Business ?

- **Builds Customer Trust:** Keeps customers informed at every key stage of their order journey.
- **Drives Repeat Purchases:** First-purchase offers encourage customers to shop again.
- **Boosts Conversions:** Timely messages and exclusive discounts motivate customers to complete more purchases.
- **Improves Customer Experience:** Delivers relevant updates through SMS and WhatsApp without manual effort.
- **Strengthens Customer Relationships:** Consistent communication helps turn first-time buyers into loyal customers.
