## 1. What is the COD Form?

The COD Form is COD King's one-page Cash on Delivery order form. A shopper taps the **COD button** on your product page, cart page or cart drawer, and the form opens as a pop-up. They enter their details, confirm, and the order is created — all without leaving the page they were on.

The form is added to your store by an app embed. It adds the COD button and loads the form only when a shopper asks for it, so it does not slow down the rest of your page.

### What your shopper sees

On the product page the shopper sees your **COD button** (here a purple **Buy with COD** under Add to cart and Buy it now). If you have a quantity offer, its tiers sit just above the buttons.

<Frame>
  ![The COD button and quantity tiers on a product page](/images/cf-51-store-product-tiers.png "The COD button on a product page")
</Frame>

Tapping the button opens the COD Form on top of the page. The shopper fills in name, phone and address, sees the order total update live, and taps **Complete order**.

  ![The COD Form open over the product page](/images/cf-52-store-form-tiers.png "The COD Form")

## 2. Why use it?

- **Fewer steps to buy.** Shoppers order from the page they are already on instead of going through a multi-step checkout.
- **Fewer fake and repeat orders.** Phone verification, order limits, block lists and bot protection are built in.
- **More revenue per order.** Quantity offers, bundles, order bumps and upsells can appear right inside the form.
- **Clean addresses.** Address suggestions while the shopper types, and fields that follow each country's address format.
- **Your brand.** Colours, texts, logo, fields and the thank-you step are all yours to change.

## 3. Where to find it

In your Shopify admin, open **COD King → Settings**. The **COD FORM** group in the settings menu has seven pages:

| Page | What it is for |
| :-- | :-- |
| [Setup](/cod-form/setup) | Turn the form on, choose where the COD button shows, countries, phone verification, address suggestions |
| [Buttons](/cod-form/buttons) | Text, colours and placement of the COD button |
| [Form designer](/cod-form/form-designer) | Fields, blocks, design, texts, thank-you step and live preview |
| [Fraud protection](/cod-form/fraud-protection) | Block lists, order limits, bot protection, return-risk checks |
| [Offers](/cod-form/offers) | Quantity offers, bundles, order bumps, upsells and more |
| [Analytics](/cod-form/analytics) | Sessions, orders, conversion funnel and breakdowns |
| [Advanced settings](/cod-form/advanced-settings) | Shipping rates, order total limits, discount codes, abandoned forms |

## 4. Recommended order to set it up

1. **[Setup](/cod-form/setup)** — make sure the app embed is on, the COD Form is on, and pick where the COD button shows.
2. **[Buttons](/cod-form/buttons)** — set the button text and colours so it matches your store.
3. **[Form designer](/cod-form/form-designer)** — choose the fields shoppers fill in, then style the form and write your thank-you message.
4. **[Fraud protection](/cod-form/fraud-protection)** — add order limits and block lists before you start taking real orders.
5. **[Offers](/cod-form/offers)** and **[Advanced settings](/cod-form/advanced-settings)** — optional extras once the basics work.
6. Place a test order from your storefront and check the **[Analytics](/cod-form/analytics)** page.

<Info>
  Your changes to the COD Form apply to the COD Form only. Other COD King checkout settings, such as Partial Payment and Prepaid Discount, are separate and are not changed.
</Info>
