**Analytics** shows how the COD Form performs on your store. Open it from **COD King → Settings → COD FORM → Analytics**.

## How to read this page in two minutes

Think of the COD Form as a small shop counter. **Sessions** is how many people walked up to the counter, **Orders** is how many bought, and **Conversion rate** is the share who bought. If your conversion rate is low, look at the **Funnel** below to see at which step people walk away.

Check this page once a week, and again a few days after every change you make (a new offer, a shorter form, a different button). Compare with the previous period so you can tell whether the change helped.

## 1. Filters and key numbers

At the top, choose the period with **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**, or set your own **From** and **To** dates. You can narrow the data by **Country**, **Device** and **Product**, and tick **Compare with the previous period** to see how the numbers moved. **Export CSV** downloads the data.

The cards show:

| Number | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Sessions** | Times the COD Form was opened. |
| **Orders** | Orders placed through the form. |
| **Conversion rate** | Orders as a share of sessions. |
| **Revenue** | Total value of those orders. |
| **Average order value** | Revenue divided by orders. |
| **Left without ordering** | Sessions that ended without an order. |
| **Abandonment rate** | Share of sessions that ended without an order. |
| **Sent to pay online** | Shoppers who chose **Pay online instead**. |

## 2. Funnel

The **Funnel** shows the steps shoppers go through: **Button views**, **Form opens**, **Started typing**, **Shipping selected**, **Submit attempts** and **Orders**. The percentage next to each step is the share of shoppers who came from the step before.

<Frame>
  ![Conversion funnel](/images/cf-34-analytics-funnel.png "Funnel")
</Frame>

<Tip>
  A big drop between **Form opens** and **Started typing** usually means the form feels too long or the first field is unclear. Try hiding fields you do not need in the [Form designer](/cod-form/form-designer#1-fields).
</Tip>

## 3. Trends and breakdowns

- **Sessions and orders per day** — a chart of both over the period.
- **By source** — product page versus cart drawer and other places the button shows.
- **By device** — desktop versus mobile.
- **By country** — sessions, orders and conversion rate per country.
- **Latest sessions** — the most recent form sessions with time, source, device, country and outcome.
