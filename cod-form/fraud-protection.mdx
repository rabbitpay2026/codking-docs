COD orders are easy to place and easy to refuse. The **Fraud protection** page helps you stop fake and repeat orders before they reach your warehouse. Open it from **COD King → Settings → COD FORM → Fraud protection**.

## Before you start: what these tools are for

With cash on delivery the buyer pays nothing up front, so some people place orders as a joke, to spite a competitor, or without any intention of accepting the parcel. Each one costs you shipping both ways. The tools on this page work like a doorman: block known bad actors, slow down repeat attempts, and keep automated scripts out.

| If this is happening | Use |
| :-- | :-- |
| The same person keeps ordering and refusing | [Block lists](#1-block-lists) |
| Someone places many orders in a short time | [Order limits](#2-order-limits) |
| Orders arrive in seconds, with strange values | [Bot protection](#3-quantity-and-bot-protection) |
| Buyers in risky areas or with a history of refusals | [Return-risk gating](#4-return-risk-gating-and-blocked-attempts) |

<Note>
  Start gently. A very strict rule can stop real customers, for example a family sharing one phone or one internet connection. Turn one rule on at a time and watch the **Blocked attempts** log before adding the next.
</Note>

## 1. Block lists

Buyers on a block list cannot place a COD order through the form. They see your message and can still pay online.

1. Choose a tab: **Phone numbers**, **Emails** or **IP addresses**.
2. Type the value in the box and click **Add to block list**.
3. Optionally, write your own **Message for blocked buyers**. Leave it empty to use the default text of the form.

Each list holds up to 5,000 entries.

<Frame>
  ![Block lists](/images/cf-28-fraud-block-lists.png "Block lists")
</Frame>

## 2. Order limits

Stop repeat fake orders by allowing at most a set number of orders in a set number of hours. You can limit by:

- **Phone number**
- **Email**
- **IP address**

For each one, tick the box and set **Maximum orders** and **Within (hours)**. For example, "3 orders within 24 hours" per phone number.

Use **Message when a limit is reached** to tell shoppers why their order was stopped. Leave it empty to use the default text.

<Frame>
  ![Order limits](/images/cf-29-fraud-order-limits.png "Order limits")
</Frame>

## 3. Quantity and bot protection

- **Maximum total quantity per order** — caps how many items one order can contain. Leave it as **No limit** if you do not want a cap.
- **Minimum seconds before submit** — a form sent faster than this is treated as a bot.
- **Use a hidden field that only bots fill in** — a trap field that real shoppers never see.

<Frame>
  ![Quantity and bot protection](/images/cf-30-fraud-quantity-bot.png "Quantity and bot protection")
</Frame>

## 4. Return-risk gating and blocked attempts

**Return-risk gating** removes full COD for buyers who are likely to refuse the parcel. They are offered partial payment (when a payment rule allows it) or **Pay online**. Tick **Remove full COD for risky buyers** to turn it on.

**Blocked attempts** is a log of every time the form refused a COD order, and why. Use the **Reason** list to filter it.

<Frame>
  ![Return-risk gating and blocked attempts](/images/cf-31-fraud-return-risk-blocked.png "Return-risk gating and blocked attempts")
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Combine a low order limit per phone number with phone verification (OTP) in [Setup](/cod-form/setup#3-set-up-phone-verification-otp) for the strongest protection against fake orders.
</Tip>
