The **Form designer** is where you build the COD Form itself. Open it from **COD King → Settings → COD FORM → Form designer**.

It has six tabs: **Fields**, **Blocks**, **Design**, **Texts**, **Thank-you** and **Form**. A **Preview** of your draft sits at the bottom of each tab, with a **Mobile / Desktop** switch.

<Info>
  The preview uses sample products and prices and shows your draft, not what is live.
</Info>

## 1. Fields

The **Fields** tab lists every field the form can ask for. For each field you can:

- drag the handle, or use the up and down arrows, to change the order,
- tick **Show** to show or hide it,
- click the pencil icon to edit it.

Fields marked **Required** must be filled in by the shopper. The phone and country fields are always part of the form.

### Contact fields

**Full name**, **Last name**, **Phone number**, **Email** and **Send me news and offers by SMS and email** (a marketing consent tick box).

By default shoppers enter their name in one **Full name** field. Turn on **Last name** to ask for first and last name separately.

<Frame>
  ![Contact fields in the Form designer](/images/cf-17-designer-fields-contact.png "Contact fields")
</Frame>

### Address fields

**Country**, **Address**, **Apartment, suite, etc.**, **Postal code**, **State / Province**, **City**, **GST number** and **Order note**.

The country field is shown only when more than one country is enabled in [Setup](/cod-form/setup). City, state and postal code follow each country's address format.

<Frame>
  ![Address fields in the Form designer](/images/cf-18-designer-fields-address.png "Address fields")
</Frame>

### Custom fields

Click **Add custom field** to ask for anything else you need, such as a delivery time or a size. Answers are saved on the order.

Tick **Ask for the phone number first** to change the order of the form: phone, then OTP, then a saved address is filled in. When it is off, the address form comes first.

This option needs phone verification. Turn on **Ask buyers to verify their phone number with a code** in [Setup](/cod-form/setup#3-set-up-phone-verification-otp) first; each code sent uses message credits. If you only want a normal form, leave **Ask for the phone number first** off and the buyer lands straight on the address form.

<Frame>
  ![Custom fields and phone-first option](/images/cf-19-designer-custom-fields.png "Custom fields")
</Frame>

With the option on, the buyer sees only a phone number box and a **Continue** button first.

<Frame>
  ![The phone-first step on the storefront](/images/cf-59-store-phone-first.png "Phone-first step")
</Frame>

## 2. Blocks

The **Blocks** tab lists the sections of the form from top to bottom. Drag them or use the arrows to reorder, and tick **Show** to turn a block on or off. **Add block** adds another block.

| Block | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Order summary** | Items in the order. Choose whether to show **Product image**, **Compare-at price** and the **Quantity selector**. |
| **Discount code** | A box for a discount code. Discount codes must also be turned on in [Advanced settings](/cod-form/advanced-settings). |
| **Quantity offer** | Shown only for products that have an active quantity offer. |
| **Contact fields** and **Address fields** | The fields from the Fields tab. These are always on. |
| **Shipping options** | The delivery rates the shopper can choose from. |
| **Order bumps** | Shown only when an order bump matches the cart. Up to 3 bumps are shown. |
| **Totals** | The order total. Tick **Show the tax line** to add a tax line. |
| **Submit button** | Always last. Tick **Show the total in the button** to put the amount on the button. |

<Frame>
  ![Form blocks](/images/cf-20-designer-blocks.png "Form blocks")
</Frame>

## 3. Design

### Theme

Pick a **Theme**:

- **Classic** — large labels beside the fields and a big order button.
- **Express** — the look of the COD King checkout: white cards, small labels above the fields and a compact button.

Choosing a theme sets its colours and corner radius. You can change them afterwards.

<Frame>
  ![Theme choice](/images/cf-21-designer-design-theme.png "Theme")
</Frame>

### Colours

Set the **Primary (button)**, **Text on the button**, **Text**, **Secondary text**, **Background**, **Panels**, **Borders** and **Errors** colours, or click **Templates** to start from a ready-made set.

<Frame>
  ![Colour settings](/images/cf-22-designer-design-colours.png "Colours")
</Frame>

### Shape and spacing

Use the **Form corner radius** and **Field corner radius** sliders, and choose the **Spacing** (for example **Comfortable**). The Express theme uses its own font.

<Frame>
  ![Shape and spacing](/images/cf-23-designer-design-shape.png "Shape and spacing")
</Frame>

### Header and behaviour

- **Show the title** — shows the form title at the top.
- **Logo URL (optional)** — shown at the top of the form. Upload your logo in **Shopify admin → Content → Files**, copy its link and paste it here. A wide logo about 260×60 px looks best.
- **Keep the submit button visible while scrolling**
- **Hide the shipping selector when only one rate applies**
- **Offer "Pay online instead" to blocked buyers**

<Frame>
  ![Header and behaviour settings](/images/cf-24-designer-design-header.png "Header and behaviour")
</Frame>

## 4. Texts

Every word in the form can be changed in the **Texts** tab.

1. Pick the **Language** you want to edit, or use **Add a language** to add a new one.
2. Use **Show the form in** to make every shopper see the form in one language, whatever language your storefront is in.
3. Choose a **Section** (for example **General**), or type in **Search all texts** to find a text quickly.
4. Edit the text in any field. The grey text is the default; leave a field as it is to keep the default.

**Reset this language to defaults** puts every text for that language back to how it was.

<Frame>
  ![Texts tab](/images/cf-25-designer-texts.png "Texts")
</Frame>

## 5. Thank-you

Under **After the order is placed**, choose what the shopper sees next:

- **Shopify order status page** — the default. If the order has no status page, the message below is shown instead.
- **Redirect to a custom URL**
- **Stay on the page and show the order details in the form (no redirect)** — the shopper sees your message, the items, the total and the delivery address in the pop-up.
- **Open WhatsApp with a prefilled message**

Write your **Message** (up to 300 characters). You can use these variables: `{order_name}`, `{order_number}`, `{order_id}`, `{total}`, `{first_name}`, `{last_name}`, `{phone}` and `{email}`.

<Frame>
  ![Thank-you settings](/images/cf-26-designer-thank-you.png "Thank-you")
</Frame>

## 6. Form

The **Form** tab names your form. The **Form name** is only for you; shoppers do not see it. **Products that use this form** lists where it applies — the default form is used for every product that has no other form.

The preview below shows the form as a shopper sees it: logo and title, order summary, discount code, address fields and the order button, with **Pay online instead** underneath.

<Frame>
  ![Form name and preview](/images/cf-27-designer-form-and-preview.png "Form name and preview")
</Frame>
