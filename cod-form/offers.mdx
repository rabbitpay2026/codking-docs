**Offers** is the home for everything that helps shoppers add more to their order, or buy at all. Think of each offer as a small shop-floor trick: "buy 3, save more", "add gift wrap for 49", "wait, here is 50 off before you go". You switch each one on from this page, and your buyers see them on the product page and inside the COD form. Open it from **COD King → Settings → COD FORM → Offers**. Each card opens a list page for that offer type, and a green badge on the card shows how many offers of that type are active.

<Frame>
  ![The Offers page](/images/cf-32-offers-hub.png "Offers")
</Frame>

Each offer type below follows the same pattern: what it is in plain words, when it is worth using, how to set it up, what your buyer actually sees on your store, and the mistakes to avoid. The store screenshots on this page were taken on a test store, so your product names, prices and colours will differ.

| Offer | What it does | Where the buyer sees it |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| [Quantity offers](#quantity-offers) | Buy more, save more with up to 5 tiers. | Product page and form |
| [Bundles](#bundles) | Sell 2 to 6 products as a set with a discount. | Product page and form |
| [Order bumps](#order-bumps) | A small add-on the buyer ticks inside the form. | Form |
| [Pre-purchase upsells](#pre-purchase-upsells) | Suggest products before the order is placed. | Pop-up or form section |
| [One-click upsells](#one-click-upsells) | Offer one more product right after the order. | Thank-you step |
| [Downsell on close](#downsell-on-close) | A discount when the buyer tries to leave the form. | Pop-up |
| [Free shipping and gift thresholds](#free-shipping-and-gift-thresholds) | A progress bar toward a reward. | Form |
| [A/B tests](#ab-tests) | Compare versions of an offer. | Split by buyer |

## Fields every offer shares

Each offer editor starts with an **Offer** card and ends with a **Products** card. These behave the same everywhere.

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Name** | A label for you. Buyers never see it. |
| **Status** | **Active** or paused. The **Pause** button on the list page does the same. |
| **Priority** | A number, lowest wins. If several offers of the same type match the same product, the one with the lowest number is used. Default is 0. |
| **Starts / Ends** (optional) | A date and time window. Leave both empty for an offer that always runs. |
| **Products** | **All products**, or **Selected products and collections** to limit the offer to the ones you pick. |

<Frame>
  ![The Offer card shared by every offer](/images/cf-39-offer-quantity-card.png "The Offer card shared by every offer")
</Frame>

<Note>
  Every offer is created and edited the same way: open the card, click **Add** (or **Edit** on an existing offer), fill in the fields, and save. Use **Pause** to switch an offer off without deleting it.
</Note>

## Quantity offers

**In plain words:** the more a buyer takes, the cheaper each piece gets. A quantity offer shows tiers such as "Buy 2, get 10% off, buy 3, get 15% off". The buyer picks a tier on the product page, and the choice carries into the COD form with the same price.

**Use it when** you want to lift the number of units per order, for example for consumables, accessories or anything buyers happily stock up on.

### What your buyer sees

On the product page, a "Buy more, save more" block appears between the quantity box and the buttons. Each tier is a card with its label, the discounted price, the original price crossed out, and an optional badge such as **Most Popular**.

<Frame>
  ![Quantity tiers on the product page, above the Buy with COD button](/images/cf-51-store-product-tiers.png "Quantity tiers on the product page")
</Frame>

When the buyer opens the COD form, the same tiers appear at the top. Picking **3 packs** updates the order summary, shows a green "You save" line, and the **Complete order** button shows the new total.

<Frame>
  ![The same tiers inside the COD form, with the saving shown](/images/cf-52-store-form-tiers.png "Quantity tiers inside the COD form")
</Frame>

### How to set it up

### Tiers

Add up to 5 tiers. Each tier has:

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Quantity** | How many units this tier is for. |
| **Discount type** | **No discount**, **Percentage**, **Fixed amount off**, or **Fixed unit price**. |
| **Discount value** | The percentage, the amount off, or the price per unit, depending on the type. |
| **Label** | The text on the tier card (up to 60 characters), such as "Buy 2". |
| **Badge** (optional) | A small tag such as "Most popular" (up to 30 characters). |
| **Image URL** (optional) | A small picture for the tier. |
| **Preselected** | The tier chosen when the page loads. Choose **No preselected tier** if you want the buyer to pick. |

**Apply a tier to higher quantities too.** When on, a buyer who orders 4 units gets the 3-unit tier price. When off, a tier only applies to its exact quantity.

<Frame>
  ![Quantity offer tiers](/images/cf-40-offer-quantity-tiers.png "Quantity offer tiers")
</Frame>

### How it looks

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Title** | Heading above the tiers. Default "Buy more, save more" (up to 80 characters, translatable). |
| **Layout** | How the tiers are drawn. **Cards** is the default. |
| **Show the tiers on the product page** | Shows the widget on the product page. If off, the tiers show only inside the form. |

<Frame>
  ![Quantity offer layout and products](/images/cf-41-offer-quantity-look.png "Quantity offer layout and products")
</Frame>

### Worked example

You sell a face serum for 500. You want buyers to take two or three.

| Tier | Quantity | Discount type | Value | Label | Badge | What the buyer pays |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | 1 | No discount |  | Buy 1 |  | 500 |
| 2 | 2 | Percentage | 10 | Buy 2 |  | 900 (450 each) |
| 3 | 3 | Percentage | 15 | Buy 3 | Most popular | 1,275 (425 each) |

Mark tier 2 as **Preselected** if you want most buyers to land on the two-pack.

<Info>
  The quantity widget on the product page also needs to be on in [Buttons](/cod-form/buttons#4-quantity-offer-widget). If your tiers show inside the form but not on the product page, check that switch and **Show the tiers on the product page**.
</Info>

### Common mistakes

- **Tiers that make no sense together.** A bigger quantity should never cost more per unit than a smaller one.
- **Forgetting "apply to higher quantities".** With it off, a buyer who raises the quantity to 4 gets no tier price, because only the exact quantities 1, 2 and 3 have one.
- **A very long label.** Labels are limited to 60 characters and badges to 30, and long text wraps awkwardly on a phone.
- **Two quantity offers on the same product.** The one with the lowest **Priority** number wins; the other is ignored.

## Bundles

**In plain words:** "frequently bought together". You group 2 to 6 products as a set, and the buyer gets a discount for taking the whole set. The buyer chooses the variant of each product, and the discount applies to every complete set.

**Use it when** products naturally go together (a phone and its case, a shampoo and conditioner) or when you want to clear slower products by attaching them to a bestseller.

**Example:** Shampoo 300 + Conditioner 300 + Serum 400 = 1,000. Set **Amount off the set** to 100 and the buyer pays 900 for all three.

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Products** | 2 to 6 products, shown in the order you pick. |
| **Discount type** and **Amount off the set** | Percentage or fixed amount taken off the set. Buyers can leave products out and pay the regular price for those. |
| **Where to show** | **On the product page**: below the COD button, with its own button that opens the form with the whole set. **Inside the COD form**: one tap adds the missing products. You can use both. |
| **Position** | Product page: **Above the COD button**. Form: **After the order summary**. |
| **Title** | Default "Frequently bought together" (up to 120 characters). |
| **Text** (optional) | A line under the title. |
| **Product page button** (optional) | Up to 60 characters. Empty means the COD button label plus the set price. |

<Frame>
  ![Bundle products and discount](/images/cf-42-offer-bundle-products.png "Bundle products and discount")
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Where the bundle shows](/images/cf-43-offer-bundle-where.png "Where the bundle shows")
</Frame>

A live preview on the right shows how the bundle will look, for example "Buy the set with COD" and "100.00 off".

### What your buyer sees

On the product page, a "Frequently bought together" box appears under the COD button. The product the buyer is looking at is already ticked and cannot be unticked. The other products in the set are ticked too, and the buyer can untick any of them. The strike-through price is the normal total, the green badge is what the buyer saves, and the button shows the price they will pay.

<Frame>
  ![Bundle on the storefront product page](/images/cf-61-store-bundle.png "Bundle on the storefront product page")
</Frame>

If **Inside the COD form** is ticked, the same box also appears inside the order form, with an **Add to my order** button that adds the missing products in one tap.

<Frame>
  ![Bundle inside the storefront form](/images/cf-62-store-bundle-form.png "Bundle inside the storefront form")
</Frame>

<Note>
  The discount applies to the complete set. Check how your store behaves when a buyer unticks a product by trying it on your own product page before you promote the offer.
</Note>

## Order bumps

**In plain words:** a tick-box add-on inside the form, like the "add gift wrap for 49" box at a shop counter. It needs one tap and no extra page. Up to 3 matching bumps are shown, in priority order.

**Use it when** the add-on is cheap, easy to understand and does not need explaining: gift wrap, express handling, a small accessory, a free gift you want buyers to notice.

### What your buyer sees

A dashed box appears in the form, above the order totals. It shows the title, the price, and the original price crossed out. The buyer ticks the box to add it.

<Frame>
  ![An order bump (here a free gift) ticked inside the COD form](/images/cf-53-store-bump.png "An order bump inside the COD form")
</Frame>

### How to set it up

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Title** | Up to 80 characters. |
| **Description** (optional) | Up to 160 characters. |
| **Image URL** (optional) | A picture next to the bump. |
| **What is added** | A **custom line** (no product needed, good for gift wrap or express handling) or a **product from my store** (uses inventory and SKU). |
| **Price** and **Compare-at price** (optional) | The price the buyer pays and a crossed-out price. |
| **Ticked by default** | The box starts ticked. |
| **Let the buyer choose a quantity** | Shows a quantity selector. |
| **Requires shipping** | Counts the line when shipping is worked out. |
| **Charge tax** | Taxes the line. On by default. |
| **Position in the form** | For example **Before the totals**. |

<Frame>
  ![Order bump settings](/images/cf-44-offer-bump.png "Order bump settings")
</Frame>

<Info>
  The **Order bumps** block must be on in the [Form designer](/cod-form/form-designer#2-blocks).
</Info>

### Common mistakes

- **Pricing the bump higher than the product.** Buyers notice. Keep it small compared to the main order.
- **Ticked by default.** It lifts the take rate, but some buyers feel tricked when they see an extra line. Use it only for things they would clearly want.
- **Using a custom line for something you stock.** A custom line does not use inventory or SKU. Choose **a product from my store** when you need stock tracked.

## Pre-purchase upsells

**In plain words:** "before you order, want to add this?" It checks what is in the cart, and if it matches your rule it suggests other products, with or without a discount.

**Use it when** you can say in one line why the second product fits the first, for example "cart has a snowboard, suggest bindings".

**If the cart has**

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Any product** or **Specific products or collections** | The rule that triggers the offer. Products already in the cart are never offered again. |
| **Minimum / Maximum cart value** (optional) | Only trigger inside this range. |

**Then offer**

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Specific products** or **Products from a collection** | What to suggest. Out-of-stock products and products already in the cart are skipped. A collection uses its first products, in the collection's own order. |
| **Products shown at most** | 1 shows one large card. 2 to 6 show a list with an **Add** button on each product. |
| **Discount type** and **Discount** | For example **Percentage off**, 10. |
| **Let the buyer choose a quantity** | Adds a quantity selector. |

<Frame>
  ![Pre-purchase upsell rules](/images/cf-45-offer-prepurchase-rules.png "Pre-purchase upsell rules")
</Frame>

**Where to show it**

| Option | What the buyer sees |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Pop-up after Complete order** | Shown once, before the order is placed. |
| **Inside the form** | A section with an **Add** button on each product, and the total updates at once. Choose its **Position in the form**, for example **Before the totals**. |

<Frame>
  ![Where the pre-purchase upsell shows](/images/cf-46-offer-prepurchase-where.png "Where the pre-purchase upsell shows")
</Frame>

Also set the **Title** (default "Wait! Add this to your order", up to 120 characters) and optional **Text** (for example "Special price, only with this order.").

### What your buyer sees

The buyer fills in the form and taps **Complete order**. Before anything is placed, a pop-up appears with your suggestion. If they tap **Add to my order**, the product joins the order at the offer price. If they tap **No thanks, complete my order**, the order goes through as it was. The pop-up is shown once, so a buyer is never asked twice.

<Frame>
  ![Pre-purchase pop-up on the storefront](/images/cf-63-store-prepurchase.png "Pre-purchase pop-up on the storefront")
</Frame>

<Warning>
  If the pop-up never appears, check the product you chose to offer. A product that is out of stock, or already in the cart, is skipped, and when nothing is left to offer the buyer's order goes straight through. Also use **Resync settings to storefront** on the COD Form setup page after you save a new offer.
</Warning>

## One-click upsells

**In plain words:** right after the buyer places the order, you offer one more product at a special price. The buyer accepts with one tap, because the address and phone are already known. Only one upsell is shown per order: the matching offer with the best priority.

**Use it when** you have a cheap, popular product that most buyers would be happy to add to a parcel that is already going out.

### What your buyer sees

The order is confirmed first (a green "Order confirmed"). Then the offer appears: a heading, the product picture and name, the new price with the old price crossed out, a countdown ("Offer ends in 118s") and two buttons. **Add to my order** adds the product to the same order. **No thanks** closes the offer and shows the order confirmation.

<Frame>
  ![The one-click upsell shown after the order is confirmed](/images/cf-56-store-upsell.png "One-click upsell after the order")
</Frame>

### How to set it up

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Product to offer** | Pick with **Change product**. |
| **Quantity** | How many units are offered. |
| **Discount type** and **Discount** | For example **Percentage**, 20. |
| **Title** | Up to 80 characters. |
| **Text** (optional) | Up to 200 characters. |
| **Accept button** / **Decline button** | Up to 40 characters each. |
| **On-screen timer** | Seconds the offer stays on screen. 0 means no timer. |

<Frame>
  ![One-click upsell settings](/images/cf-47-offer-upsell.png "One-click upsell settings")
</Frame>

The buyer can still accept for a few minutes after ordering. That window is set by **One-click upsell window (minutes)** in [Advanced settings](/cod-form/advanced-settings) (5 by default). The COD fee is not recalculated and no extra message is sent when an upsell is accepted.

## Downsell on close

**In plain words:** a last attempt to save the sale. When the buyer tries to close the form without ordering, a small pop-up offers a discount.

**Use it when** you see many buyers open the form and leave. It works best with a small, clear discount you can afford on every order.

### What your buyer sees

When the buyer taps the close button (×), a pop-up appears instead of closing the form. **Claim discount** applies the discount and returns to the form. **No thanks** lets the buyer leave.

<Frame>
  ![The downsell pop-up shown when the buyer tries to close the form](/images/cf-54-store-downsell.png "Downsell on close")
</Frame>

After the buyer claims it, the discount shows as its own line in the order totals, named after your offer title, and the **You save** total grows.

<Frame>
  ![The downsell discount in the order totals](/images/cf-55-store-downsell-applied.png "The claimed discount in the totals")
</Frame>

If a buyer who has already seen the offer tries to close the form again, the form asks "Leave without ordering?" with **Continue my order** and **Leave**.

<Frame>
  ![The Leave without ordering confirmation](/images/cf-58-store-leave.png "Leave without ordering?")
</Frame>

### How to set it up

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Show after this many close attempts** | 1 shows the offer the first time the buyer tries to close. |
| **Discount type** and **Discount** | For example **Percentage**, 10. |
| **Title** | Default "Wait! Here is 10% off" (up to 80 characters). The title is also used as the name of the discount line in the totals, so keep it short and meaningful. |
| **Text** (optional) | Up to 200 characters, for example "Complete your order now and save." |
| **Accept button** / **Decline button** | Up to 40 characters each. |
| **On-screen timer** | Seconds, 0 for none. |

<Frame>
  ![Downsell on close settings](/images/cf-48-offer-downsell.png "Downsell on close settings")
</Frame>

## Free shipping and gift thresholds

**In plain words:** a progress bar that says "spend a little more and get free shipping (or a free gift)". Buyers who are close to the amount often add one more item to reach it, so your average order grows.

**Use it when** you want to lift your average order value without cutting prices. Set the amount about 20 to 30 percent above what most buyers spend today.

<Warning>
  Creating the threshold is not enough. The bar only shows in the form after you add the **Free shipping / gift progress bar** block in the [Form designer](/cod-form/form-designer#2-blocks): open **Blocks**, click **Add block**, choose **Free shipping / gift progress bar**, pick your threshold offer, click **Done** and then **Save**. Then use **Resync settings to storefront** on the COD Form setup page.
</Warning>

| Field | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Reward** | **Free shipping** or **A free gift**. |
| **Order amount to reach** | For example INR 1000. |
| **Gift product** | For a gift: pick it with **Change product**. One unit is added at no charge. |
| **Text while below the amount** | Type <code>\{remaining}</code> where the amount still missing should appear, for example "Add \{remaining\} more to get a free gift". Up to 120 characters. |
| **Text once reached** | Shown after the goal, for example "You unlocked free gift". Up to 120 characters. |

<Frame>
  ![Free shipping and gift threshold](/images/cf-49-offer-threshold.png "Free shipping and gift threshold")
</Frame>

### What your buyer sees

Near the bottom of the form, under the totals, the buyer sees a bar with a message such as "Add Rs. 1,214.05 more for free shipping". The bar fills as the order total grows, and the numbers under it show where the buyer is now and the amount to reach. When the goal is reached, the message changes to your "Text once reached".

<Frame>
  ![Progress bar on the storefront form](/images/cf-64-store-threshold.png "Progress bar on the storefront form")
</Frame>

<Note>
  The bar counts the order total after discounts, so a buyer using a quantity offer or a bundle needs to reach the amount after that saving.
</Note>

## A/B tests

**In plain words:** you are not sure which offer works better, so you let your buyers decide. Half of them see offer A and half see offer B, and Analytics shows which one earned more. Each buyer sees one version for the whole visit, so nobody sees the offer change while they shop. Compare up to four versions.

**Use it when** you want to settle a question with data, for example "does 10 percent off beat 100 rupees off?". Test one change at a time, and let the test run until you have a good number of orders, not just a few.

On the storefront there is nothing extra for the buyer to see: each buyer simply gets the version they were assigned.

1. Create two or more offers of the same kind.
2. Click **New A/B test**.
3. Enter a **Name** and choose the **Kind of offer** (for example **Quantity offers**).
4. In **Variants**, choose which offer each variant shows. **Show nothing (control)** is a version with no offer.
5. Set each **Traffic share**. Shares must add up to 100 %. **Split evenly** divides them equally, and **Add variant** adds another (up to four).
6. Save the test.

<Frame>
  ![New A/B test form](/images/cf-50-offer-abtest-new.png "New A/B test form")
</Frame>

Results are in [Analytics](/cod-form/analytics).

## How discounts combine

When several discounts could apply to one order, they apply in this order:

1. quantity offer,
2. then downsell,
3. then discount code.

Whether a discount code can be combined with offers is set in [Advanced settings](/cod-form/advanced-settings#3-form-behaviour-and-discount-codes). The **Open Setup** button on the Offers page takes you to the related settings.
