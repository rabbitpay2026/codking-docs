**Offers** is the home for everything that helps shoppers add more to their order. Open it from **COD King → Settings → COD FORM → Offers**. Each card opens its own page, and a green badge shows how many offers of that type are active.

<Frame>
  ![The Offers page](/images/cf-32-offers-hub.png "Offers")
</Frame>

## 1. The offer types

| Offer | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Quantity offers** | Buy more, save more: tiers with a percentage, a fixed discount or a fixed unit price. |
| **Bundles** | Sell 2 to 6 products together with a discount on the set, on the product page and in the form. |
| **Order bumps** | A small add-on the buyer can tick inside the form. Up to 3 bumps are shown. |
| **Pre-purchase upsells** | A pop-up after **Complete order**: when the cart has certain products, offer others, with or without a discount. |
| **One-click upsells** | Offer one more product right after the order. Accepting adds it to the same order. |
| **Downsell on close** | A discount shown once when the buyer tries to close the form. |
| **Free shipping and gift thresholds** | A progress bar towards free shipping or a free gift. |
| **A/B tests** | Compare up to four versions of an offer and see which one earns more. |

## 2. How discounts combine

When several discounts could apply to one order, they apply in this order:

1. quantity offer,
2. then downsell,
3. then discount code.

Whether a discount code can be combined with offers is set in [Advanced settings](/cod-form/advanced-settings#3-form-behaviour-and-discount-codes). The **Open Setup** button on this page takes you to the related settings.

<Info>
  Quantity offers also show on the product page when the **Quantity offer widget** is turned on in [Buttons](/cod-form/buttons#4-quantity-offer-widget). Order bumps appear in the form when the **Order bumps** block is on in the [Form designer](/cod-form/form-designer#2-blocks).
</Info>
