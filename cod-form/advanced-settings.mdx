**Advanced settings** holds the optional behaviour of the COD Form. The defaults suit most stores. Open it from **COD King → Settings → COD FORM → Advanced settings**.

## Which settings matter most

You can leave everything here on its default and the form works. These are the ones merchants most often change:

| Setting | Change it when |
| :-- | :-- |
| [Shipping rates](#1-shipping-rates) | Your delivery price depends on the buyer's location or the order. |
| [Order total limits](#2-order-total-limits) | You want to refuse COD on very small or very large orders. |
| **COD only through the form** | You want every COD order to arrive with the form's checks (fraud rules, OTP, offers). |
| **Abandoned forms** | You want to follow up with people who typed a phone number but did not order. |
| **One-click upsell window** | You want buyers to have more (or less) time to accept the upsell after ordering. |

<Note>
  After you change a setting that affects what buyers see, use **Resync settings to storefront** on the COD Form setup page, then reload your store to check it.
</Note>

## 1. Shipping rates

**Shipping rates** are the delivery options and prices shoppers see in the COD Form. The card shows how many of your rates are active (for example "1 of 1 rates active"). Click **Manage shipping rates** to add or edit them.

## 2. Order total limits

Offer the COD Form only when the order total is inside a range.

- **Minimum order total** and **Maximum order total** — leave a field empty for no limit.
- **Message when the total is out of range (optional)** — up to 200 characters.

## 3. Form behaviour and discount codes

Under **Form behaviour**:

- **COD only through the form** — hides Cash on delivery at Shopify checkout so COD orders always come through the form.
- **Charge tax on the COD fee** — the fee itself is set in **COD fees** and shows as its own line in the form.

Under **Discount codes**:

- **Let buyers enter a Shopify discount code** — the **Discount code** block must also be on in the [Form designer](/cod-form/form-designer#2-blocks).
- **Discount codes combine with offers** — when off, a code replaces quantity-offer and downsell discounts when it is better for the buyer.

Under **Pay online**, keep **Show "Pay online instead" in the form** ticked to give shoppers a way to pay now.

## 4. Abandoned forms, upsell window and privacy

- **Abandoned forms** — a form with a phone or email and no order after 15 minutes counts as abandoned.
  - **Send my abandoned-cart messages for abandoned forms** — works only when abandoned-cart messages are on in **Customer notifications**.
  - **Create a Shopify draft order for each abandoned form**
  - **Discount in the recovery link** — optionally add a discount to the recovery link.
- **One-click upsell window (minutes)** — how long after the order a buyer can accept a one-click upsell.
- **Embedded form on the product page** — tick **Show the form inline on the product page instead of a popup**.
- **Respect the buyer's cookie consent for tracking** — marketing pixels and funnel analytics follow Shopify's Customer Privacy settings.
- **Hide "Powered by COD King"** — removes the footer line from the form.
