# COD to Prepaid

COD-to-prepaid workflows are designed to give eligible customers an opportunity to choose a prepaid payment journey instead of Cash on Delivery.

## Prepaid incentives

COD King includes prepaid incentive capabilities as a product area. The exact discount configuration, eligibility conditions, and customer experience will be documented from verified product screens.

<Tip>
  When detailed setup guides are added, test incentive rules carefully before using them for live customers.
</Tip>
