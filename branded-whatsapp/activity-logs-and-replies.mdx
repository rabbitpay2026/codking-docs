# Checking Activity Logs and Understanding Replies

Once your WhatsApp number is connected, COD King shows customer replies in the **WhatsApp inbox**, and past activity is available from the **Business Brand WhatsApp** page.

## Where to find activity

- **WhatsApp inbox**: Go to **COD King → Settings → Notifications → WhatsApp inbox**. Every customer message shows here, so your team can reply from COD King.
- **Past activity**: On the **Business Brand WhatsApp** page, click **View past activity**.
- **Analytics**: Go to **COD King → Analytics**.

The inbox on the dev store shows an empty state until a number is connected. It has an **AI agent** button and an **Inbox settings** button in the top right.

## How replies appear

After your number is connected, each customer message appears in the inbox. Your team can reply from COD King.

## Configuration options

| Option | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbox settings | WhatsApp inbox | Controls how the inbox works |
| AI agent | WhatsApp inbox | Opens the AI agent for replies |
| View past activity | Business Brand WhatsApp | Shows past WhatsApp activity |
| WhatsApp Approval Status | Customer notifications | Shows the approval status of your messages |

## Practical example

A merchant sends an abandoned cart reminder. A customer replies "Is COD available?" in WhatsApp. The reply appears in the WhatsApp inbox, and the team can answer it from COD King.

## Verification

- Send a test message from a connected number and confirm it appears in **View past activity** or in the inbox.
- Reply from your test phone and confirm the reply shows in the inbox.

## Troubleshooting

- **The inbox is empty.** Connect your WhatsApp number first.
- **You cannot find past messages.** Check **View past activity** on the Business Brand WhatsApp page.

## Important notes and limitations

- Do not share customer phone numbers when you capture screenshots of logs.
- Confirm message statuses in the app before you rely on them for support decisions.
