# Campaign Management with COD King

WhatsApp campaigns let you send an offer or announcement to a group of customers. Each campaign has an audience, an approved message, and a send time.

## How campaigns work

The campaigns page describes the flow as: pick an audience, choose an approved template, and send now or schedule it. Every message is tracked from sent to read and reply.

<Frame>
  ![WhatsApp campaigns page with Create campaign disabled and Setup needed status](/images/bw-03-campaigns-setup-needed.png "WhatsApp campaigns page: Create campaign (1) and Setup needed status (2)")
</Frame>

1. **Create campaign** is disabled until setup is complete.
2. **Setup needed** shows that the number and message are not ready yet.

## Prerequisites: set up campaigns

The **Set up WhatsApp campaigns** checklist has three steps:

1. **Connect your WhatsApp Business number.** Messages are sent from your own number. Click **Connect number**.
2. **Get a message approved.** WhatsApp only allows pre-approved messages for campaigns. Click **Choose a message** to start from a ready-made one.
3. **Bring in your customers.** Click **Import from store** to copy your Shopify customers and abandoned checkouts, or **Upload a list**.

## Ready-made campaigns

Each ready-made campaign comes with an audience and a message. You can change anything before you send.

| Campaign | Audience | Status on the dev store |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Win back lapsed customers | Customers who have not ordered in 60 days | Message needs approval |
| Festive sale | Everyone subscribed | Message needs approval |
| Turn COD buyers into online payers | COD buyers who never paid online, with no returns or RTO | Message needs approval |
| Recover abandoned checkouts | Shoppers who left a checkout 1 to 7 days ago and didn't finish | Message needs approval |

## Audiences

The campaigns page has two tabs, **Campaigns** and **Audiences**. Use the **Open audiences** button to manage audiences.

## Practical example: a win-back campaign

A store wants to bring back customers who have not ordered in 60 days.

1. Open **Settings → WhatsApp campaigns**, and finish the three setup steps.
2. Under **Start from a ready-made campaign**, open **Win back lapsed customers**.
3. Wait until the message is approved. The **Use this** button is enabled after approval.
4. Review the audience (customers who have not ordered in 60 days) and the message, then send or schedule.
5. Check the results in the activity logs (see [Checking Activity Logs and Understanding Replies](/branded-whatsapp/activity-logs-and-replies)).

## Verification

- Confirm the campaign audience matches the ready-made description before sending.
- Send a test to your own number first, if your setup allows it.

## Troubleshooting

- **Create campaign is disabled.** Finish the setup checklist. The number and an approved message are required.
- **Use this is disabled.** The message is waiting for approval.
- **No customers are in the audience.** Import customers from your store or upload a list.

## Important notes and limitations

- Campaigns can only use approved messages.
- Campaign messages are charged at the WhatsApp marketing rate. On the dev store this is $0.0116 per message. See [Introduction and How It Works](/branded-whatsapp/introduction).
- Do not assume features exist because other WhatsApp marketing tools offer them. Check the app first.
