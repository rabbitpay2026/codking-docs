Business Brand WhatsApp sends your customer messages from **your own verified WhatsApp Business number**, so shoppers see your brand name instead of a generic sender. COD King connects the number, then sends a WhatsApp template message each time an order event happens: order placed, COD verification, shipped, delivered and more.

## Open the page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open COD King → Settings">
    In your Shopify admin, open the COD King app and click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Business Brand WhatsApp">
    It is under **Notifications** in the left menu. The page is headed "Send order updates from your own verified WhatsApp number."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The dashboard

<Frame>
  ![Business Brand WhatsApp page showing the connected number, the Manage account link and the last 7 days message counts](/images/wa-01-main-top.png "The arrows point to the connected number and its badges, the Manage account link and the last 7 days message counts")
</Frame>

| Area | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Number card** | Your WhatsApp number and verified business name, with a **Verified** badge and a **Quality** rating from WhatsApp (for example **Quality: high**). **Manage account** opens the [account page](/business-whatsapp/connect-number). |
| **Last 7 days** | **Messages sent**, **Delivered**, **Read** and **Failed** counts. Click **View** next to **Failed** to see which messages failed. The links **Inbox**, **AI agent** and **View activity** sit above the counts. |
| **Customer notifications** | One row per event, with a counter such as "3 of 14 turned on". **Manage all** opens the [notification list](/business-whatsapp/notifications). |
| **Campaigns** and **My templates** | Shortcuts to create a campaign or a template. See [Templates](/business-whatsapp/templates). |

<Info>
  The **Quality** rating comes from WhatsApp, not from COD King. It reflects how customers react to your messages. Keep it high by sending only messages customers expect.
</Info>

## Customer notifications

<Frame>
  ![List of customer notifications with Live badges, Pause and Edit links and Set up buttons](/images/wa-02-notifications.png "Each row is one event. Live rows show Pause and Edit. Rows that are not set up show Set up")
</Frame>

Each row shows the event, what triggers it and, once it is on, a **Live** badge, the template it sends, how many messages went out this week, and the **Read** and **Replied** rates for the last 30 days. The **COD to prepaid** row also shows a **Clicked** rate.

| Notification | When it fires |
| --- | --- |
| **Order placed** | Every time a new order is placed. |
| **Order placed — partial payment** | When an order is placed as a partial payment (advance paid online, balance on delivery). If it has no template, the normal **Order placed** template is used. |
| **COD order verification** | After a cash on delivery order is placed. The customer taps **Confirm** to proceed or **Cancel** to cancel the order. |
| **COD verification reminder** | Reminds the customer to confirm a COD order they have not answered yet. |
| **COD to prepaid** | Invites a COD customer to pay online instead. |
| **Order fulfilled** | When an order is fulfilled and has tracking info. |
| **Out for delivery** | When the courier marks the shipment out for delivery. Needs your shipping integration. |
| **Order delivered** | When the courier marks the shipment delivered. Needs your shipping integration. |
| **Feedback request** | Asks for feedback or a review, sent 1 day after delivery. |
| **Reorder reminder** | Reminds the customer to buy again, sent 30 days after delivery. |
| **Order cancelled** | When an order is cancelled in Shopify (by you, by auto-cancel or by the customer). |
| **First-time purchase** | Once per customer, on their first ever order with your shop. |
| **Abandoned cart** | When a customer enters checkout but does not complete the order. |
| **OTP message** | Sends the one-time verification code before checkout. |

## Campaigns and templates

<Frame>
  ![Campaigns card with New campaign, All campaigns and Audiences, and My templates card with the approved count](/images/wa-03-templates-campaigns.png "The arrows point to New campaign, the approved templates count and Manage templates")
</Frame>

The two cards at the bottom of the page are shortcuts. **My templates** shows how many templates WhatsApp has approved, with **New template** and **Manage templates**. The **Campaigns** card links to **New campaign**, **All campaigns** and **Audiences**, which belong to WhatsApp campaigns and are not covered on this page.
