OTP (One-Time Password) Verification is a security and validation layer that confirms the authenticity of a customer’s order by verifying their mobile number using a one-time code sent via SMS or WhatsApp.

## What is OTP Verification ?

OTP Verification is a process where customers receive a unique, time-sensitive code on their mobile number, which they must enter to confirm their identity and order.

This ensures that:

- The phone number is valid and reachable
- The customer genuinely intends to place the order
- Fake or spam orders are reduced

---

## Why OTP Verification is Needed ?

For COD businesses, unverified orders can directly impact profitability and operations. OTP verification helps solve these key challenges:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="High Return to Origin (RTO)" icon="truck-return">
    Up to **20%–40%** of COD orders can fail due to fake or unverified customers. OTP verification ensures only genuine buyers place orders, significantly reducing RTO losses.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fake & Spam Orders" icon="shield-x">
    Invalid phone numbers, bots, and prank orders increase operational risk. OTP verification filters out non-serious users before the order is confirmed.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Low Customer Intent" icon="user-x">
    Without verification, customers may place orders casually without commitment. OTP confirmation ensures higher intent and improves order reliability.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Delivery Failures" icon="map-pin-off">
    Incorrect or unreachable contact details lead to failed deliveries. OTP verification validates the customer’s phone number before processing the order.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## One Pass Verification

One Pass Verification streamlines the order confirmation process by verifying customers in a single, seamless step—reducing friction while maintaining strong security.

### Step 1: Enable One Pass Verification

Go to your dashboard and navigate to **Verification Settings**. Select **One Pass Verification** as your preferred method.

### Step 2: Choose Verification Channel

Select the channel for sending verification (SMS or WhatsApp) based on your customer preference and reach.

### Step 3: Configure Trigger Conditions

Set when One Pass Verification should be applied—such as for all orders, COD orders, or specific risk-based scenarios.

### Step 4: Customize the Verification Message

Create a clear and concise message that includes your brand name and verification intent to build trust.

### Step 5: Activate Auto-Verification Logic

Enable automatic verification so that once the customer completes the action (e.g., clicking the link or confirming), the order is instantly marked as verified.

### Step 6: Save & Enable

Save your settings and turn on One Pass Verification.

### Step 7: Test the Flow

Place a test order to ensure the verification process works smoothly and completes in a single step.

<Info>
  One Pass Verification acts as a _single-step trust checkpoint_, confirming genuine customers instantly while eliminating the need for multiple OTP verifications—reducing friction and improving conversion rates.
</Info>

**Step 6: Customize Verification Flow**<br />Edit messaging, branding, and user prompts to ensure a smooth and consistent checkout experience.

**Step 7: Save & Publish**<br />Save your changes and publish the checkout to make OTP verification live.

**Step 8: Test the Checkout Flow**<br />Run a test order to ensure OTP is sent, verified, and the checkout completes without issues.
