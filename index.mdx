# COD King Documentation

COD King helps Shopify merchants manage and optimize their **Cash on Delivery (COD)** workflows from one place. These guides help you configure your store, manage COD features, improve customer engagement, and connect local SMS providers.

Use the sections below to quickly find the feature or integration you need.
