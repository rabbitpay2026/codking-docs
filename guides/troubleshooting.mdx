If you experience any issues while using COD King, first check that your settings and configurations are correctly saved.

If the issue continues, our support team is available to help you troubleshoot and resolve the problem.

## Contact Support

For assistance, contact our support team by email:

**Email:** [support@notifiktech.com](mailto:support@notifiktech.com)

When contacting support, briefly describe the issue and include your Shopify store URL so our team can assist you more quickly.

You can also visit our [Contact Us page](https://codking.tech/contact) for additional support options.
