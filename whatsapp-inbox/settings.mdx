Inbox settings control how the inbox behaves for every conversation. Open it from **Inbox settings** at the top of the [WhatsApp inbox](/whatsapp-inbox/overview).

Most settings are saved with the **Save** button at the top right of the page. The button stays grey until you change something. **Saved replies** and **Labels** save right away.

## General

<Frame>
  ![Inbox settings General section with Send read receipts and Label conversations automatically ticked, and the start of the Welcome message section](/images/wi-05-settings-general.png "The arrows point to the two General options, the Save button and the welcome message option")
</Frame>

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send read receipts** | Customers see blue ticks when someone on your team opens their conversation. |
| **Label conversations automatically** | Adds **Campaign reply**, **Notification reply** and **Cancel request** labels based on what the customer replied to or wrote. |

## Welcome message

The welcome message is sent automatically the first time a customer messages you. It is not sent if your team replied in the last 6 hours.

<Frame>
  ![Welcome message box with a short greeting and the customer detail buttons, followed by the Away message section](/images/wi-06-welcome-away.png "The arrows point to the welcome message text, the customer detail buttons, the away message option and the away message text")
</Frame>

Tick **Send a welcome message**, then write the text. It can be up to 1,024 characters.

Under **Insert customer details**, click a button to add a field that fills in for each customer: **Customer name**, **First name**, **Order number**, **Order status**, **Tracking link**, **Order amount** or **Store name**. They appear in the text as placeholders such as `{{first_name}}`.

<Tip>
  Keep the welcome message short and set an expectation, for example "We'll reply shortly". The default text does this.
</Tip>

## Away message

The away message is sent automatically when a customer writes outside your business hours. COD King sends at most one automatic reply every 6 hours to the same customer. Tick **Send an away message** and write the text. It uses the same customer detail buttons and the same 1,024 character limit.

## Business hours

<Frame>
  ![Business hours section with Open all the time selected, followed by the Keyword replies section](/images/wi-07-hours-keywords.png "The arrows point to the two business hours options and the Add keyword reply button")
</Frame>

Business hours are used by the away message and by the [unanswered conversation email](#unanswered-conversation-email).

- **Open all the time.** Every message counts as inside business hours, so no away message is sent.
- **Set business hours.** Choose your opening times for each day.

<Frame>
  ![Business hours with Set business hours selected showing a row for each day with Closed tick box and start and end times](/images/wi-08-business-hours.png "The arrows point to Set business hours, the start and end times and the Closed tick box")
</Frame>

Each day has a **Closed** tick box and a start and end time. The screenshot shows 09:00 to 18:00 from Monday to Saturday with Sunday closed. Times follow your Shopify store's time zone, which is shown under the table.

## Keyword replies

Keyword replies answer automatically when a customer's message contains a word you choose. They are checked first, then the away message, then the welcome message. So a keyword reply wins over both.

<Frame>
  ![A keyword reply rule with the Keywords box, the match type, the Reply box and the customer detail buttons](/images/wi-09-keyword-reply.png "The arrows point to the Keywords box, the match type, the Reply box, the On tick box and the delete button")
</Frame>

Click **Add keyword reply** to add a rule.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Keywords** | The words to look for, for example `track` or `where is my order`. Press Enter or type a comma after each keyword. You can add up to 10. |
| **Match type** | Set to **Message contains the word** by default. |
| **Reply** | The message sent back, up to 1,024 characters. Use the customer detail buttons to add the order number, tracking link and more. |
| **On** | Tick to keep the rule active. Untick to pause it without deleting it. |
| **Delete (bin icon)** | Removes the rule. |

<Info>
  A good first rule: keywords `track, where is my order`, reply with the **Tracking link** button. The Reply box is empty until you write it.
</Info>

## Unanswered conversation email

<Frame>
  ![Unanswered conversation email option with a 30 minutes without a reply setting, followed by the Saved replies section with one reply called tracking](/images/wi-10-email-saved-replies.png "The arrows point to the email option, the wait time, the saved reply buttons and Add saved reply")
</Frame>

When this is on, COD King emails the assigned teammate, or the store owner if nobody is assigned, when a customer waits too long during your business hours. Tick **Send an email when a customer is waiting** and choose how long to wait in **Send after**. The screenshot shows **30 minutes without a reply**.

## Saved replies

Saved replies are ready-made answers shared with your whole team. In a conversation, type `/` in the reply box and pick a saved reply to use it.

Each saved reply has a name and a shortcut. The example in the screenshot is named **tracking**, with the shortcut `/tracking`. Click the pencil to edit it, the bin to delete it, or **Add saved reply** to create a new one. Saved replies save right away.

## Labels

<Frame>
  ![Labels section listing the seven built-in labels with a pencil edit icon and an Add label button](/images/wi-11-labels-settings.png "The arrows point to a built-in tag, the edit pencil and the Add label button")
</Frame>

Labels sort your conversations. The seven built-in labels are marked **Built-in**. Click the pencil to edit one, or **Add label** to create your own. Changes here save right away.
